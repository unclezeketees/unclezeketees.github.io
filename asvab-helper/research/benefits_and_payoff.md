# What the Coast Guard Gives an Enlisted Member in Return (2025-2026): Education, Pay, College Credit, Life Quality, Post-Service

Research date: 2026-11-09. Access note: most Coast Guard-hosted pages on mycg.uscg.mil, forcecom.uscg.mil and dcms.uscg.mil returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool during this session (bot/geo block). Where a fact comes from a search-engine excerpt of one of those pages rather than a full read, it is labeled "(search excerpt only)". Official Coast Guard ALCOAST messages mirrored on content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG were readable and are the primary sources used.

## Key Question 1: Education benefits (Tuition Assistance, Post-9/11 GI Bill, college credit for training, CLEP/DSST, degree partnerships)

### Takeaway
While serving, an active-duty Coast Guard enlisted member can get up to $4,500 per fiscal year of tuition paid (capped at $250 per semester hour) with no service-obligation payback for enlisted, and after 36 months of service earns the full Post-9/11 GI Bill: 36 months of in-state public tuition, a monthly housing allowance pegged to E-5-with-dependents BAH at the school's location, and up to $1,000 a year for books. A-school and other formal training can also appear on the Joint Services Transcript with ACE college-credit recommendations, and DANTES-funded CLEP/DSST exams are free to Coast Guard members.

### Cited Findings

**Coast Guard Tuition Assistance (FY26 policy, ALCOAST 390/25, released Sept 18, 2025, effective Oct 1, 2025)**
- Tuition funded "not to exceed $250.00 per semester hour, $166.67 per quarter hour, or $16.67 per clock hour"; annual cap "$4,500.00 per member" per fiscal year. — [ALCOAST 390/25 FY26 Coast Guard Tuition Assistance Policy (govdelivery mirror)](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/3f33eba)
- "Lifetime credit hour limits for TA are lifted." — [ALCOAST 390/25](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/3f33eba)
- Enlisted members incur no service obligation for TA, but the class must finish before the enlistment ends; active-duty officers owe 24 months after the last TA course. — [ALCOAST 390/25](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/3f33eba)
- Covered: first and second academic certificates, first and second associate degrees, first and second bachelor's degrees, first master's degree, and associate-to-master's bridge programs; nothing above a master's. — [ALCOAST 390/25](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/3f33eba)
- Eligibility conditions for active duty: unit and watch-station qualifications, proficiency of craft, conduct in the six months before class, and performance ratings; enlisted with a "not recommended" (NR) mark are ineligible. — [ALCOAST 390/25](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/3f33eba)
- Procedural rules: application to ETQC 7 to 90 days before class start; evaluated degree plan required before a second course is funded; must keep a 2.0 GPA after 15 undergraduate semester hours; reimbursement owed if the course is not completed with a C or better (undergraduate). Fees are not covered by TA. — [ALCOAST 390/25](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/3f33eba)
- Third-party school pages still list older caps ($2,250, $3,750, $4,000); the FY26 official message's $4,500 supersedes them. — Example of outdated figure: [Idaho State Univ. Coast Guard TA page](https://www.isu.edu/registrar/military-ed-benefits/coast-guard-tuition-assistance/) vs. [ALCOAST 390/25](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/3f33eba)
- Prior-year reference showing the trend: FY24 TA policy was ALCOAST 340/23 (Sept 2023). — [ALCOAST 340/23 FY24 TA Policy](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/37179b9)

**Post-9/11 GI Bill (Chapter 33) - VA official**
- Up to 36 months of entitlement; pays in-state public tuition and fees in full at the 100% tier (private/foreign schools capped), a monthly housing allowance for students enrolled more than half time, a books-and-supplies stipend, plus tutorial assistance, licensing/certification test reimbursement, and work-study. — [VA: About the Post-9/11 GI Bill](https://www.va.gov/education/about-gi-bill-benefits/post-9-11/)
- Eligibility starts at 90 days of active duty after Sept 10, 2001; service that ended on or after Jan 1, 2013 has no expiration date ("Forever GI Bill"). — [VA: About the Post-9/11 GI Bill](https://www.va.gov/education/about-gi-bill-benefits/post-9-11/)
- Benefit tiers (academic year Aug 1, 2026 to July 31, 2027): 36+ months active duty = 100%; 30-35 months = 90%; 24-29 = 80%; 18-23 = 70%; 6-17 = 60%; 90 days to 5 months = 50%. — [VA: Post-9/11 GI Bill rates](https://www.va.gov/education/benefit-rates/post-9-11-gi-bill-rates/)
- Private-school tuition cap $30,908.34 per academic year (2026-27). — [VA: Post-9/11 GI Bill rates](https://www.va.gov/education/benefit-rates/post-9-11-gi-bill-rates/)
- Books and supplies: up to $1,000 per academic year ($41.67 per credit hour, up to 24 credits), prorated by tier. — [VA: Post-9/11 GI Bill rates](https://www.va.gov/education/benefit-rates/post-9-11-gi-bill-rates/)
- Monthly housing allowance (MHA): based on the 2026 BAH rate for an E-5 with dependents at the location where the student attends most classes; online-only students get half the national average, capped at $1,198/month; foreign schools capped at $2,396/month. — [VA: Post-9/11 GI Bill rates](https://www.va.gov/education/benefit-rates/post-9-11-gi-bill-rates/)
- Transfer to dependents: "Qualified service members can transfer all 36 months or part of their benefits to a spouse or child"; the Defense Department (for Coast Guard, DHS) must approve; dependents must be in DEERS. — [VA: About the Post-9/11 GI Bill](https://www.va.gov/education/about-gi-bill-benefits/post-9-11/); DEERS detail from [MyAirForceBenefits Post-9/11 GI Bill fact sheet](https://myairforcebenefits.us.af.mil/Benefit-Library/Federal-Benefits/Post-9/11-GI-Bill?serv=22)
- Transfer eligibility rules reported by secondary sources conflict: James Madison Univ. says transfer is limited to members with less than 16 years of service (effective 7/20/19) — [JMU transfer page](https://www.jmu.edu/registrar/veterans/transferbenefits.shtml); The Military Wallet says the member must be able to serve four more years — [The Military Wallet](https://themilitarywallet.com/?p=301); a 2026 guide says at least 6 years of service at the request date — [BestMilitaryResume 2026 transfer guide](https://bestmilitaryresume.com/blog/career-transition/gi-bill-transfer-rules-2026-eligibility-guide). These are not mutually exclusive (DoD policy has historically required 6 years served + 4 more years agreed, and a 16-year cap), but no single primary source confirming the current Coast Guard-specific rule was retrievable.
- A spouse generally cannot receive MHA while the member is still on active duty; children can. — [MyAirForceBenefits Post-9/11 GI Bill fact sheet](https://myairforcebenefits.us.af.mil/Benefit-Library/Federal-Benefits/Post-9/11-GI-Bill?serv=22) (labeled: DoD-hosted, not Coast Guard-specific)

**College credit for A-school and ratings (Joint Services Transcript / ACE)**
- ACE "provides quality assurance and policy guidance for the Joint Services Transcript (JST) used by the Army, Marine Corps, Navy, and Coast Guard"; each college decides what ACE recommendations it accepts. — [ACE: Understanding the Joint Services Transcript](https://www.acenet.edu/Programs-Services/Pages/Credit-Transcripts/Understanding-the-Joint-Services-Transcript.aspx)
- Only ACE-evaluated training with recommended credit appears on the JST summary; Coast Guard training needs an official course identification number to appear. — [JST FAQ (jst.doded.mil)](https://jst.doded.mil/faq.html)
- ACE's sample Coast Guard JST lists an "Information Systems Technician 'A' School" among evaluated training, showing rating A-schools can carry ACE credit recommendations. — [ACE Coast Guard JST Sample (PDF)](https://newwww.acenet.edu/Documents/Coast-Guard-JST-Sample.pdf)
- The Coast Guard's JST page is hosted at forcecom.uscg.mil (ETQC Voluntary Education). — [FORCECOM ETQC JST page](https://www.forcecom.uscg.mil/Our-Organization/FORCECOM-UNITS/ETQC/VOLUNTARY-EDUCATION/Joint-Services-Transcript) (returned 403; content not read)

**CLEP/DSST**
- DANTES funds CLEP and DSST exams for active-duty, Reserve and Guard members including Coast Guard, and Coast Guard spouses are covered through Coast Guard funding; DANTES-funded CLEP candidates receive the official study guide free. — [DANTES Credit-by-Exam fact sheet, Jan 10, 2024 (PDF)](https://www.dantes.mil/Portals/132/Marketing%20Resources/Factsheets/CBE.10JAN24.pdf); [DANTES CLEP page](https://www.dantes.mil/CLEP)
- The Coast Guard ETQC Voluntary Education office (customer service (757) 343-1660) administers TA and credentialing. — [FORCECOM ETQC Voluntary Education, Active Duty](https://www.forcecom.uscg.mil/Our-Organization/FORCECOM-UNITS/ETQC/VOLUNTARY-EDUCATION/Active-Duty/) (search excerpt only; page returned 403)

**Degree partnerships**
- Recruiting site states the GI Bill, tuition assistance "and other programs can fund college education" and that the Coast Guard offers "hands-on training and professional certifications." — [gocoastguard.com: Receive Pay & Benefits](https://www.gocoastguard.com/active-duty-careers/enlisted-opportunities/receive-pay-benefits)
- Several universities market Coast Guard-specific programs (e.g., Purdue Global "Coast Guard College Education Resources and Special Programs"). — [Purdue Global Coast Guard page](https://purdueglobal.edu/military/coast-guard) (labeled: vendor marketing, not an official Coast Guard partnership statement)

### Inferences
- For a 17-year-old, the concrete education math is: a 4-year enlistment can fund roughly $18,000 of TA tuition while serving (4 x $4,500) with no payback obligation, then a 100%-tier GI Bill worth full in-state tuition plus housing for up to 36 months (four academic years) afterward.
- Because ACE evaluates A-schools individually and longer technical schools (e.g., IT A-school, the longest in the Coast Guard at about 31 weeks per the Petaluma page excerpt) tend to carry more evaluated content, a technical rating earned via a higher ASVAB composite plausibly yields more transferable JST credit; this is an inference, since no per-rating credit table was retrievable.

### Gaps
- FY27 TA policy (expected ~Sept 2026) was not found; the FY26 message is the latest confirmed. The FY26 message does not state a minimum time-in-service or "after A-school" rule for new members to start TA; the forcecom Active-Duty page that may contain this returned 403.
- No current official Coast Guard page confirming CLEP/DSST funding rules was readable (DANTES fact sheet used instead).
- No per-rating ACE credit-hour table was retrievable; ACE Military Guide course lookups would be needed.
- "Coast Guard Institute" as an entity: the function now sits under FORCECOM ETQC Voluntary Education; no page explicitly describing an active "Coast Guard Institute" degree partnership was retrievable.

## Key Question 2: Pay progression (2026 basic pay E-1 to E-5, time to each grade, advanced enlistment grade, BAH/BAS, enlistment bonuses)

### Takeaway
A 2026 recruit starts at $2,226/month (E-1 under 4 months), is automatically an E-2 ($2,698) on boot-camp graduation, can enter as an E-3 ($2,837) with college credits/JROTC/Sea Cadet-type experience, and, since a Nov 2023 policy change, can be advanced to E-4 ($3,142+) immediately on A-school graduation with no time-in-grade wait, on top of tax-free housing and a $476.95/month food allowance. FY2026 enlistment bonuses stack up to a statutory $75,000 cap, with up to $60,000 for a critical rating, up to $15,000 for college credits, and up to $10,000 for shipping quickly.

### Cited Findings

**2026 basic pay (monthly, active component; 3.8% raise effective Jan 1, 2026)**
- 3.8% raise effective January 1, 2026. — [Military.com 2026 pay charts](https://www.military.com/benefits/military-pay/charts) (third party, cites DFAS); [NavyCS 2026 pay chart](https://navycs.com/charts/2026-military-pay-chart.html) (third party)
- E-1 under 4 months: $2,226; E-1 4+ months: $2,407 (flat at all years). — [NavyCS 2026 pay chart](https://navycs.com/charts/2026-military-pay-chart.html); E-1 $2,407.20 confirmed by [Military.com 2026 pay charts](https://www.military.com/benefits/military-pay/charts)
- E-2: $2,698 (flat; $2,697.90). — [NavyCS](https://navycs.com/charts/2026-military-pay-chart.html); [Military.com](https://www.military.com/benefits/military-pay/charts)
- E-3: $2,837 (<2 yrs), $3,015 (over 2), $3,198 (over 3 and beyond). — [NavyCS](https://navycs.com/charts/2026-military-pay-chart.html); $2,836.80 at over 4 per [Military.com](https://www.military.com/benefits/military-pay/charts)
- E-4: $3,142 (<2), $3,303 (over 2), $3,482 (over 3), $3,659 (over 4), $3,815 (over 6). — [NavyCS](https://navycs.com/charts/2026-military-pay-chart.html); $3,142.20 over 4 column per [Military.com](https://www.military.com/benefits/military-pay/charts) (note: Military.com's "Over 4" E-4 figure of $3,142.20 matches NavyCS's "<2" figure, suggesting Military.com's column labels on that page are offset; treat NavyCS's finer table as the working set and verify against DFAS)
- E-5: $3,343 (<2), $3,598 (over 2), $3,776 (over 3), $3,947 (over 4), $4,110 (over 6); top of E-5 scale $4,421.70. — [NavyCS](https://navycs.com/charts/2026-military-pay-chart.html); [Military.com](https://www.military.com/benefits/military-pay/charts)
- Pay is by grade, not branch: a Coast Guard E-5 is paid the same basic pay as an Army or Navy E-5 with the same years. — [VA Loan Network base pay explainer](https://valoannetwork.com/military-base-pay/) (third party)
- Official DFAS/militarypay.defense.gov tables were not fetched directly (not attempted after repeated .mil blocks); label all pay figures above as third-party transcriptions of the DFAS table.

**Time-in-service to each grade (Coast Guard specific)**
- Coast Guard enlistees advance to E-2 automatically on graduating boot camp; one guide calls the Coast Guard "the only service that automatically advances members to E-2 upon graduation from basic training." — [Military.com Coast Guard enlisted ranks](https://mst.military.com/coast-guard/enlisted-ranks.html) (third party)
- E-2 to E-3 normally requires about six months in grade plus command approval; some enlistees reach E-3 at boot-camp graduation (e.g., six-year enlistment or prior experience). — [Military.com Coast Guard ranks guide](https://mst.military.com/coast-guard/ranks.html) (third party)
- Nov 29, 2023 (ALCOAST 473/23): the Coast Guard removed the time-in-grade requirement for advancement to E-4 on A-school graduation; "Commanding Officers of Training Centers may advance E-2 or E-3 members to E-4 upon graduation from A-School," provided the member has satisfactory advancement eligibility (E-3s need a CO/OIC recommendation). Message self-cancels Nov 28, 2024 pending incorporation into COMDTINST M1000.2C. — [ALCOAST 473/23 (govdelivery mirror)](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/37d6228)
- Non-rates who attend A-school do not take the servicewide exam for E-4; third class petty officer is earned on graduation. — [Military.com Coast Guard ranks guide](https://mst.military.com/coast-guard/ranks.html) (third party)
- Under the Vested Crewmember program, recruits with a guaranteed A-school are assigned to a rated E-4 billet from boot camp and no longer must serve 4 months as a non-rate before A-school (see Key Question 3). — [ALCGENL 225/23 (govdelivery mirror)](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/37551c9)

**Advanced enlistment grade (E-2/E-3) in the Coast Guard**
- Official recruiting site: "Recruits with certain experience, such as college credits, JROTC, ROTC, police or sea explorer programs, or scouting achievements, may be advanced to E-3 right after boot camp." Referral bonuses, Sea Cadets and Eagle Scout are not named on this page (scouting is mentioned generally). — [gocoastguard.com Hiring Incentives](https://gocoastguard.com/hiring-incentives)
- Historical Recruiting Manual rule (per BCMR decisions): 30 semester hours = E-2, 60 semester hours = E-3, but the wording is permissive and the Coast Guard has at times suspended the 60-credit E-3 rule or limited E-3 entry to bachelor's-degree holders (FY15/2016). — [BCMR 2017-075](https://boards.law.af.mil/CG/BCMR/Enlisted%20Advancement%20and%20DOR/2017-075%20Advancement_Redacted.pdf); [BCMR 2018-177](https://boards.law.af.mil/CG/BCMR/Enlisted%20Advancement%20and%20DOR/2018-177%20Advancement_Redacted.pdf) (dated 2015-2018; current Recruiting Manual not retrievable)
- Separately, a College Credits Enlistment Bonus for recruits with 30+ cumulative credit hours was announced in 2022. — [ALCOAST 403/22 (dcms PDF)](https://dcms.uscg.mil/Portals/10/CG-1/PPC/ALCOAST%20403-22.pdf) (search excerpt only; PDF returned 403)

**BAH and BAS**
- 2026 enlisted BAS: $476.95/month (up 2.4% from $465.77 in 2025); officer BAS $328.48; BAS is tax-free. — [The Military Wallet 2026 BAS rates](https://themilitarywallet.com/bas-rates/) (third party; the DFAS BAS page at militarypay.defense.gov returned 403 to fetch but is listed as the source)
- BAH varies by ZIP code and dependency status; 2026 BAH rates rose an average of 4.2% nationwide. — [Military.com 2026 Guide to Pay and Allowances](https://365.military.com/benefits/military-pay/2026-guide-pay-and-allowances-military-service-members-veterans-and-retirees.html) (third party)
- The recruiting site describes pay as "basic pay and non-taxable allowances," with other allowances or bonuses depending on job and station. — [gocoastguard.com Receive Pay & Benefits](https://www.gocoastguard.com/active-duty-careers/enlisted-opportunities/receive-pay-benefits)

**Enlistment bonuses (FY2026)**
- ALCOAST 386/25 (Sept 12, 2025) authorizes Enlistment Bonuses (EB), Designated Career Field Bonuses (DCFB, formerly Critical Skill Training Bonus, previously capped at $20,000), and a Guaranteed "A" School Bonus (GA-EB); the Accessions Service Center may stack multiple enlistment bonuses "not to exceed the statutory cap of $75,000 per recruit." Specific FY26 amounts are on an internal SharePoint page, not in the public message. — [ALCOAST 386/25 FY2026 Workforce Planning Team Results (govdelivery mirror)](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/3f25a0a)
- Members receive the DCFB amount in effect on their A-school convening date. — [ALCOAST 386/25](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/3f25a0a)
- Official recruiting site (read 2026-11-09): "Up to $60,000 to affiliate with a critical rating"; "Up to $15,000 for higher education credits"; "Up to $10,000 to ship expeditiously"; "Up to $15,000 for Reserve assignments"; availability varies with recruiting needs. — [gocoastguard.com Hiring Incentives](https://gocoastguard.com/hiring-incentives)
- Recruiting site banner: "Get up to a $75,000 Bonus." — [gocoastguard.com Receive Pay & Benefits](https://www.gocoastguard.com/active-duty-careers/enlisted-opportunities/receive-pay-benefits)
- Third-party comparison (Sept 15, 2026): Coast Guard stacked max $75,000 is second only to the Navy's nuclear-field bonus (up to $140,000); Army critical-specialty max $50,000 + $10,000 quick-ship; Air Force up to $40,000; Marines up to $15,000 + $5,000-$10,000 ship bonus. — [ClearanceJobs: From $15K to $140K](https://news.clearancejobs.com/2026/09/15/from-15k-to-140k-the-military-jobs-offering-big-enlistment-bonuses/) (third party)
- A Coast Guard news item titled "Do you qualify for FY2026 bonuses?" exists (mycg.uscg.mil, article 4305181) but returned 403. — [MyCG FY2026 bonuses article](https://www.mycg.uscg.mil/News/Article/4305181/do-you-qualify-for-fy2026-bonuses/)
- In 2026 the Coast Guard launched a Bonus Accountability and Tracking System (BATS) (ALCOAST 087/26, Apr 2026). — [ALCOAST 087/26 (govdelivery)](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/4116a95) (title only; not read)
- Conflicting/outdated: one bonus guide lists a Coast Guard maximum of $20,000 (typical $5,000-$20,000, top jobs ET and IS). — [MilitaryPrepHub](https://www.militaryprephub.com/bonuses.html) (third party; inconsistent with the official $75,000 cap and $60,000 critical-rating figure)

### Inferences
- Combining the above: an E-1 recruit who enters as E-3 via college credits earns about $611/month more than an E-1 ($2,837 vs $2,226) from day one, and reaching E-4 on A-school graduation (now possible without a time-in-grade wait) adds another ~$305/month, plus the E-4 pay scale rises with years of service while E-1/E-2 pay is flat.
- Because the Guaranteed "A" School Bonus and DCFB are tied to specific ratings and A-school convening, the bonus money a recruit can actually claim depends heavily on qualifying (by ASVAB composite) for the critical rating's A-school.

### Gaps
- Exact FY2026 bonus dollar amounts per rating are on an internal Coast Guard SharePoint and were not public; only the "up to" figures from the recruiting site are available.
- Current Recruiting Manual rule on E-2/E-3 entry for 30/60 credits or JROTC/Sea Cadets could not be confirmed from a 2025-2026 primary document; only the recruiting-site statement and 2015-2018 BCMR decisions were found.
- Official DFAS pay tables not fetched; BAH example figures for specific Coast Guard duty stations not collected.
- Whether ALCOAST 473/23 (E-4 on A-school graduation) was incorporated into the current COMDTINST M1000.2 revision after its Nov 2024 self-cancel date was not confirmed.

## Key Question 3: How ASVAB performance affects outcomes (rating choice, guaranteed A-school, Vested Crewmember)

### Takeaway
The Coast Guard explicitly ties a guaranteed A-school out of boot camp to ASVAB scores ("If ASVAB scores qualify for a high-demand specialty, the school may be guaranteed"), and the Petaluma IT school page quotes a higher "guaranteed school" composite (175 composite / AR 60) than the minimum to attend (172 / AR 52). Recruits with a guaranteed A-school now skip the non-rate waiting period and are assigned straight to an E-4 billet under the Vested Crewmember program, which has expanded beyond its original four ratings.

### Cited Findings
- Official recruiting site: "Guaranteed specialty training: If ASVAB scores qualify for a high-demand specialty, the school may be guaranteed immediately after boot camp." — [gocoastguard.com Hiring Incentives](https://gocoastguard.com/hiring-incentives)
- TRACEN Petaluma IT A-school page lists a composite of MK + EI + GS = 172 plus either AR 52 or AFCT 65, and gives a "175/60" combination for guaranteed school; IT A-school runs about 31 weeks, the longest in the Coast Guard; students must be eligible for a SECRET clearance and have normal color perception. — [FORCECOM TRACEN Petaluma IT training page](https://www.forcecom.uscg.mil/Our-Organization/FORCECOM-UNITS/TraCen-Petaluma/Training/IT) (search excerpt only; page returned 403). Cross-check against the separate rating-cutoff research before use.
- Oct 2021: Coast Guard adjusted A-school ASVAB requirements effective Nov 1, 2021 so more members can attend the school of their choosing, described as the first major ASVAB policy change since 2004; composite requirements for all A-schools except diver were adjusted by 10 points; the service already granted 300+ merit-based composite waivers annually. — [MyCG: ASVAB score requirements changing for A-schools](https://www.mycg.uscg.mil/News/Article/2816942/asvab-score-requirements-changing-for-a-schools/) (search excerpt only; 403); mirrored as [PPC: Coast Guard ASVAB Policy Changes, Flag Voice 563](https://www.dcms.uscg.mil/ppc/news/Article/2816424/coast-guard-asvab-policy-changes-flag-voice-563/) (403)
- 2016 Coast Guard ASVAB Process Guide: a CO/OIC may waive up to five points of the total of two or more subtest scores for a Class "A" school, but a minimum AFQT score cannot be waived. — [USCG ASVAB Process Guide, Feb 22, 2016 (PDF)](https://www.uscg.mil/Portals/3/Documents/VOL_ED/ASVAB_Process_Guide_22FEB2016.pdf) (dated 2016)
- Vested Crewmember Beta Test (ALCGENL 225/23, Oct 11, 2023): recruits with guaranteed BM, DC, ET or GM A-school are assigned at boot camp to a rated Third Class Petty Officer position in their future rating rather than a non-rate position; the test "supersedes the requirement that non-rated members serve 4 months at their first unit before attending A-school"; orders execute on boot-camp graduation and a Type II A-school convening date is assigned by CGRC, which commands may not delay "without just and due cause." — [ALCGENL 225/23 (govdelivery mirror)](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/37551c9)
- The expanded Vested Crewmember Program covers additional ratings including BM, CS, DC, EM, ET, GM (list truncated in the excerpt). — [DCMS Gear & Grit: The training path from coxswain to conn](https://www.dcms.uscg.mil/Gear-Grit-Blog-View/Article/3817184/the-training-path-from-coxswain-to-conn/) (search excerpt only; 403)
- Oct 2023 MyCG commentary "Coast Guard streamlines guaranteed A-school process" describes the delays the program fixed (recruits had to start boot camp in a narrow window before A-school, and applicants walked away). — [MyCG article 3553964](https://www.mycg.uscg.mil/News/Article/3553964/coast-guard-streamlines-guaranteed-a-school-process/) (search excerpt only; 403)
- A Guaranteed "A" School Bonus (GA-EB) is among the FY2026 authorized enlistment incentives. — [ALCOAST 386/25](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/3f25a0a)
- Older third-party claim that the Coast Guard "rarely, if ever" guarantees a job in enlistment contracts predates the 2023 program and is contradicted by the current recruiting site. — [Dummies.com ASVAB article](https://www.dummies.com/article/academics-the-arts/study-skills-test-prep/armed-services/how-asvab-scores-determine-military-training-programs-and-jobs-142396/) (dated, third party) vs. [gocoastguard.com Hiring Incentives](https://gocoastguard.com/hiring-incentives)
- July 2026: ALCOAST 213/26 created a pathway for E-2 through E-4 members to Diver "A" school, showing rating pathways continue to change. — [ALCOAST 213/26 (govdelivery)](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/41fd0bc) (title only)

### Inferences
- The payoff chain for scoring higher is concrete: qualifying composite -> eligible for a critical/high-demand rating -> guaranteed A-school in the contract -> Vested Crewmember E-4 billet from day one (skipping the 4-month non-rate wait and years-long A-school waitlists) -> eligibility for the Guaranteed A-School Bonus and DCFB. A recruit who only clears the AFQT floor has none of these.
- The existence of a separate, higher "guaranteed school" score (175/60 vs 172/52 for IT) means a few extra ASVAB points can be the difference between "may attend someday" and "guaranteed in writing."

### Gaps
- The full current rating list for the expanded Vested Crewmember Program was not retrievable (article 403, excerpt truncated).
- No official statement quantifying "more rating choices" by score was found; the link is inferred from composite-score gating plus the recruiting site's conditional guarantee language.
- Whether the IT 172/175 figures on the Petaluma page already reflect the 2021 10-point adjustment is unconfirmed.

## Key Question 4: Life quality (leave, medical/dental, housing, first assignments, deployment patterns, retention/culture)

### Takeaway
Enlisted members earn 2.5 days of leave a month (30 days a year) and get full medical, dental and vision coverage for themselves and dependents; first units are typically small boat stations or cutters, and cutter patrols in 2025-2026 ran roughly 54 to 129 days. The Coast Guard is widely described (opinion, not a verified statistic) as having the best retention in the military, but a 2025 GAO report shows it lost more enlisted members than it recruited every year FY2019-2023 and ended FY2024 about 2,600 enlisted (8.5%) short.

### Cited Findings

**Leave, medical, housing**
- "30 days of paid vacation annually"; "Full medical, dental, and vision coverage for you and your dependents"; also discounted life insurance, retirement plan options, financial counseling, mental health support and legal services. — [gocoastguard.com Receive Pay & Benefits](https://www.gocoastguard.com/active-duty-careers/enlisted-opportunities/receive-pay-benefits)
- "Every servicemember earns two-and-a-half days of annual leave every month totaling 30 days each year"; emergency leave for enlisted may be granted up to 60 days at a time. — [USCG Sea Legs: Pay and Benefits](https://dcms.uscg.mil/Our-Organization/Assistant-Commandant-for-Human-Resources-CG-1/Health-Safety-and-Work-Life-CG-11/Office-of-Work-Life-CG-111/Sea-Legs/Pay-Benefits) (search excerpt only; 403 on fetch)
- BAS (food) $476.95/month tax-free in 2026; BAH (housing) by location (see Key Question 2 sources).

**Typical first assignment and deployment patterns**
- After boot camp at Training Center Cape May, NJ (the only Coast Guard boot camp), the first unit is typically a small boat station (29-, 45-, 47-foot boats) or a cutter (Fast Response Cutter up to National Security Cutter); graduates usually arrive as undesignated non-rates and qualify on the job while awaiting A-school. — [HonestMOS Coast Guard BM E1-E3 playbook](https://www.honestmos.com/playbook/coast-guard/bm/e1-e3) (third party)
- Policy baseline: non-rated members served 4 months at their first unit before A-school; superseded for guaranteed-A-school recruits by the Vested Crewmember program. — [ALCGENL 225/23](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/37551c9)
- Cutter patrol lengths, 2025-2026: USCGC Waesche 113-day Bering Sea patrol — [DVIDS](https://www.dvidshub.net/news/printable/566047); USCGC Midgett 129-day deployment (50 days on Bering Sea patrol) — [USCG News](https://news.uscg.mil/Press-Releases/Article/3643795/coast-guard-cutter-midgett-returns-to-home-port-after-a-129-day-alaska-patrol); USCGC Stratton 3-month deployment — [USCG News](https://www.news.uscg.mil/Press-Releases/Article/4446697/coast-guard-cutter-stratton-returns-to-california-following-3-month-deployment/); USCGC Alert 60-day Florida Straits patrol — [USCG News](https://www.news.uscg.mil/Press-Releases/Article/3990535/coast-guard-cutter-alert-completes-first-patrol-after-relocating-to-east-coast/); USCGC Campbell 54-day patrol — [MilitarySpot](https://www.militaryspot.com/news/coast-guard-cutter-returns-after-54-day-patrol)
- GAO's measure of National Security Cutter tempo: a goal of 185 to 230 "days away from home port" per year. — [GAO report on cutter crewing (PDF)](https://www.gao.gov/assets/670/669046.pdf)
- A Coast Guard commentary on sea duty describes extended underway time as a recurring strain on crews. — [MyCG: A homage to sea duty](https://www.mycg.uscg.mil/News/Article/2495513/a-homage-to-sea-duty-in-the-coast-guard-successes-challenges-and-being-underway/) (search excerpt only; 403)

**Retention and culture**
- GAO-25-107869 (published Apr 23, 2025; released May 14, 2025): the Coast Guard lost more enlisted members than it recruited each year FY2019-FY2023 (FY2023: >3,800 lost vs 3,126 recruited); FY2024 reversed with gains exceeding losses by 1,000+, but the service ended FY2024 about 2,600 enlisted (8.5%) short of target and projected ~1,600 short at end of FY2026; in March 2025 it said it likely would meet targets in calendar 2026. — [GAO-25-107869 full report](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-25-107869/index.html)
- GAO grouped members' stated retention issues into four categories: rotations and support services (housing, health, childcare); work environment and culture (workload, op tempo, leadership, infrastructure); compensation and career advancement; incentives and benefits. In FY2024, 67% of enlisted losses were retirements/releases, 11% officer conversions, 23% discharges. The report does not compare Coast Guard retention with other services. — [GAO-25-107869](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-25-107869/index.html)
- OPINION/PRESS CHARACTERIZATION: Federal News Network describes Coast Guard retention as usually "in the mid to upper 90 percent range" and leaders have claimed the highest retention among the armed services. — [Federal News Network](https://federalnewsnetwork.com/?p=1732250) (press characterization, not a verified statistic)
- The Coast Guard retains women at a lower rate than men; one summary: 83.9% of male officers remain after five years vs 78.3% of women, gap widening at 10 years. — [Georgetown GIWPS: Why do women leave the Coast Guard](https://giwps.georgetown.edu/resource/why-do-women-leave-the-coast-guard-and-what-can-be-done-to-encourage-them-to-stay); [Military Times 2019](https://militarytimes.com/news/your-navy/2019/04/18/why-do-so-many-women-leave-the-coast-guard) (2019 data)
- Under the Blended Retirement System, DoD estimates about 85% of members receive a government retirement benefit after 2 years of service (TSP matching vests). — [GAO-25-107869](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-25-107869/index.html)

### Inferences
- Deployment tempo depends heavily on unit type: a major cutter crew should expect multi-month patrols (roughly 2 to 4 months in the 2025-2026 examples), while small boat stations are shore-based with duty rotations; a rating choice therefore shapes daily life as much as pay (e.g., aviation ratings serve at air stations, IT/ET often at shore units or cutters).
- The "best retention in the military" reputation is widely repeated but is an opinion/press claim; the GAO data show real strain (op tempo, housing, childcare) that a prospective recruit should weigh.

### Gaps
- No official source for the standard length of a first enlisted sea tour or the duty rotation at small boat stations was retrievable.
- Air station deployment patterns (e.g., Aviation Survival Technician / AMT duty cycles) were not researched within the call budget.
- No current official side-by-side retention table across services exists in the sources found; only the GAO Coast Guard-only report and press characterizations.
- TRICARE plan specifics (costs, Prime vs Select) were not pulled; only the recruiting-site statement of full coverage.

## Key Question 5: Post-service (veteran status, rating-to-civilian credential mapping, Coast Guard COOL)

### Takeaway
Coast Guard members earn GI Bill and VA eligibility on the same terms as other armed forces, and the Coast Guard's own COOL program (cool.osd.mil/uscg) plus Credentialing Assistance funding let members earn civilian licenses while serving; AMT/AET experience is FAA-recognized toward the Airframe and Powerplant certificate, DC welding certifications are reimbursed, and the Military-to-Mariner program maps sea-going ratings to Merchant Mariner Credentials.

### Cited Findings
- Veteran status/GI Bill: Coast Guard service counts as active duty for Post-9/11 GI Bill eligibility tiers (the VA page lists eligibility by active-duty days without excluding the Coast Guard, and Coast Guard JSTs/ACE are integrated with the other services). — [VA: About the Post-9/11 GI Bill](https://www.va.gov/education/about-gi-bill-benefits/post-9-11/); [ACE JST page](https://www.acenet.edu/Programs-Services/Pages/Credit-Transcripts/Understanding-the-Joint-Services-Transcript.aspx)
- CG COOL "offers service-training pathways to obtain numerous credentials, certifications, and licenses"; it is an online tool for Coast Guard members to find credentials related to their work; the Voluntary Credentialing Program is governed by COMDTINST 1540.10 (Jan 10, 2020). — [MyCG: Credentialing your Coast Guard experience](https://mycg.uscg.mil/News/Article/2349131/credentialing-your-coast-guard-experience) (search excerpt only; 403); [COMDTINST 1540.10 (PDF)](https://media.defense.gov/2020/Jan/13/2002233571/-1/-1/0/CI_1540_10.PDF)
- Credentialing Assistance is available to active duty, reserve and civilian members; a vendor reports funding of up to $4,500 per fiscal year for active duty. — [MedCerts Coast Guard COOL page](https://medcerts.com/tuition/coast-guard-cool-credentialing-assistance-programs?lang=en) (vendor; official forcecom CA User Guide and FAQs exist at [CA User Guide, May 20, 2022](https://www.forcecom.uscg.mil/Portals/3/Documents/ETQC/CG%20COOL/CA%20User%20Guide_Final%2020%20May%202022_1.pdf) and [CA FAQs](https://www.forcecom.uscg.mil/Portals/3/Documents/ETQC/CG%20COOL/CA%20FAQs_Final%2020%20May%202022.pdf) but returned 403)
- Rating example: for Damage Controlman, CG COOL reimburses training needed for welding certifications. — [MyCG: Get the 101 on the Coast Guard's Voluntary Credentialing Program (Oct 2023)](https://www.mycg.uscg.mil/News/Article/3559689/get-the-101-on-the-coast-guards-voluntary-credentialing-program/) (search excerpt only; 403)
- AMT -> FAA A&P: Coast Guard AMT (and legacy AD/AE/AM codes) experience is listed as creditable toward the FAA Airframe and Powerplant certificate; AET is creditable for Airframe and/or Powerplant subject to an FAA ASI evaluation; crediting is governed by 14 CFR 65.77 (list current as of Oct 2012, page updated Oct 14, 2025). — [AirframeAndPowerplant.com Coast Guard crosswalk](https://airframeandpowerplant.com/m/coastguard.html) (third party reproducing FAA list)
- Merchant Mariner Credential: the National Maritime Center is the Coast Guard's merchant mariner credentialing authority; the Military-to-Mariner (M2M) program counsels service members on credential options and analyzes military training that aligns with merchant mariner credentials, with course collections that each have a pathway to an MMC. — [MyCG: New courses developed by the Military to Mariner program (2021)](https://www.mycg.uscg.mil/News/Article/2578919/new-courses-developed-by-the-military-to-mariner-program-and-approved-by-the-na/) (search excerpt only; 403)
- COOL listing example: a QMED (Qualified Member of the Engineering Department) credential must be endorsed separately for each QMED rating. — [COOL credential listing CERT_NQ6295](https://dod-civ-cool-review.s3.amazonaws.com/search/CERT_NQ6295.htm)
- The GI Bill reimburses licensing and certification tests after service as well. — [VA: About the Post-9/11 GI Bill](https://www.va.gov/education/about-gi-bill-benefits/post-9-11/)
- Military.com summary of Coast Guard credentialing programs (third party). — [Military.com Coast Guard Credentialing Programs](https://www.military.com/education/finding-a-school/coast-guard-credentialing-programs.html)

### Inferences
- The civilian-credential payoff is rating-dependent, so the ASVAB composite that opens a technical rating (AMT, ET, IT, EM, DC) also opens a specific licensed civilian career path (A&P mechanic, electronics/IT certs, electrician, welder), while deck ratings (BM) and engineering ratings (MK) feed Merchant Mariner Credentials via M2M.

### Gaps
- No single official document mapping each Coast Guard rating to specific credentials (e.g., HS -> EMT, BM/MK -> specific MMC endorsements) was retrievable; the cool.osd.mil/uscg site was not fetched and the forcecom COOL pages returned 403.
- The official Credentialing Assistance annual cap could not be confirmed from a Coast Guard source (only a vendor's $4,500 figure).
- HS-to-EMT/NREMT mapping: not found in retrievable sources.
