## Key question 1: What does each third-party table actually say, rating by rating (formula, minimum, subtest minimums, AFQT alternative, date)?

### Takeaway
Six third-party sources publish a per-rating table (MIL, OMK/OMK2, HERO-L, HERO-S, HMOS, WIKI) and one (TN) publishes a partial, source-attributed list. Peterson's, JobTestPrep, EnlistIQ Prep, MilitarySpot and Study.com either publish no per-rating table or could not be reached. No two full tables agree on both formula and value for more than a handful of ratings.

### Inferences
- OMK, HERO-L, HERO-S and MIL share one numeric lineage (AET/ET/IT 171, AMT 213, AST 162, DC/EM 152, GM 208, HS 154, MK 150, MST 115, PA 110, SK/OS/CS/YN 106, PS/BM 101, ME 100). OMK is the earliest-dated carrier (June 2024) of that lineage among the pages checked; HERO (2026) and MIL (dated Jul 2026) appear to reproduce it with formulas bolted on, and the formulas differ from site to site while the numbers stay identical, which is what you would expect if the numbers were copied and the formulas were guessed.
- MIL's values for OS (105), PA (109), SK (105), MST (114) and IS (100) break from that lineage and instead match the WIKI values, so MIL appears to be a splice of two lineages.
- HMOS matches nothing else for most technical ratings (ET/IT/CMS 222, EM 210, DC/MK 195, MST 209) and should be treated as an independent, unverifiable dataset.

## Key question 2: Which third-party values are consistent with the official anchors (IT 162/AR 52/AFQT 65 post-Nov 2021, 172 before; ME VE+AR >= 100; CMS AR+VE >= 110; Diver not reduced in 2021)? Which sources are using pre-2021 values or mixing eras?

### Takeaway
Not one third-party full table carries the post-November-2021 IT value (162); the 171/172 cluster found in MIL, OMK, HERO and WIKI is the pre-2021 value (WIKI exactly, the others off by one). ME = 100 is the only anchor every table agrees with, and both sources that list CMS (MIL 171 via AR+MK+EI+GS, HMOS 222) contradict the MyCG anchor of AR+VE >= 110.

### Cited Findings
- IT anchor check. WIKI gives "IT Information Systems Technician MK+EI+GS>=172 & AR>=52 or AFQT>=65" — the pre-2021 value with the correct subtests, AR floor and AFQT alternative — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Services_Vocational_Aptitude_Battery). Test Ninjas shows the same 172 on the TRACEN Petaluma IT page (captured April 2026) and the 162 figure only in the October 2021 MyCG commentary, and concludes the school pages "were never updated" — [Test Ninjas](https://test-ninjas.com/coast-guard-jobs-by-asvab-score).
- IT in the 2024-2026 tables: MIL "AR + MK + EI + GS = 171 (with a minimum AR Score of 52)" — [Military.com](https://www.military.com/asvab/asvab-and-coast-guard-jobs); OMK "Min. score: 171 (Minimum AR 52)" — [OMK](https://www.operationmilitarykids.org/asvab-scores-for-coast-guard-jobs/); HERO-L "MK+EI+GS 171+" and body text "IT (Information Systems Technician) = MK + EI + GS >= 171" — [ASVAB Hero ratings list](https://www.asvabhero.com/coast-guard-ratings-list); HERO-S "AR+MK+EI+GS 171, AR >= 52" — [ASVAB Hero score page](https://www.asvabhero.com/coast-guard-asvab-score); HMOS "AR+MK+EI+GS 222" — [HonestMOS](https://www.honestmos.com/tools/asvab-jobs-by-score); Peterson's "minimum AR 52, minimum overall score 171" — [Peterson's](https://www.petersons.com/blog/what-jobs-do-i-qualify-for-with-my-asvab-score-requirements/). None gives 162 or the AFQT 65 alternative for IT.
- ET anchor-adjacent check: MIL/OMK/HERO-S give an AFQT alternative of 66 for ET; WIKI and the TRACEN Petaluma ET page (per Test Ninjas) give 65 — [Military.com](https://www.military.com/asvab/asvab-and-coast-guard-jobs); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Services_Vocational_Aptitude_Battery); [Test Ninjas](https://test-ninjas.com/coast-guard-jobs-by-asvab-score).
- ME anchor check: VE+AR = 100 in MIL, HERO-L, HERO-S, WIKI, and "Min. score: 100" in OMK; Test Ninjas attributes "VE+AR 100" to the MLEA ME school page captured May 2026 but warns it may be a pre-2021 figure — sources as above and [Test Ninjas](https://test-ninjas.com/coast-guard-jobs-by-asvab-score). HMOS instead lists ME as "AFQT 40" — [HonestMOS](https://www.honestmos.com/tools/asvab-jobs-by-score).
- CMS anchor check: MIL "CMS Cyber Mission Specialist AR + MK + EI + GS = 171 (plus additional cyber-specific testing)" — [Military.com](https://www.military.com/asvab/asvab-and-coast-guard-jobs); HMOS "CMS Cyber Mission Specialist AR+MK+EI+GS 222" — [HonestMOS](https://www.honestmos.com/tools/asvab-jobs-by-score). OMK, HERO-L, HERO-S and WIKI do not list CMS at all. Neither published CMS value matches the MyCG anchor AR+VE >= 110.
- Diver check: MIL "DV Diver VE = 104 with a minimum MC Score of 50"; OMK "DV Min. score: 104"; HERO-S "DV VE+AR 104"; HMOS "DV AFQT 40"; HERO-L and WIKI omit DV — sources as above. No third-party source states a pre-/post-2021 diver value, so the "not reduced in 2021" anchor cannot be tested against them beyond noting that 104 is the only value in circulation.
- HS era check: WIKI "VE+MK+GS+AR>=207 & AR>=50" and the TRACEN Petaluma HS page (June 2026 capture, per Test Ninjas) "VE+MK+GS+AR 207, with a minimum AR of 50" versus MIL/OMK/HERO-L "VE+MK+GS 154", HERO-S "VE+MK 154", HMOS "VE+MK+GS 156" — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Services_Vocational_Aptitude_Battery); [Test Ninjas](https://test-ninjas.com/coast-guard-jobs-by-asvab-score); [Military.com](https://www.military.com/asvab/asvab-and-coast-guard-jobs); [ASVAB Hero score page](https://www.asvabhero.com/coast-guard-asvab-score); [HonestMOS](https://www.honestmos.com/tools/asvab-jobs-by-score).

### Inferences
- Every full third-party table is pre-2021 in substance. WIKI is a clean pre-2021 table (its IT/ET/HS/ME rows match the un-updated official school pages Test Ninjas captured). The MIL/OMK/HERO cluster looks like a pre-2021 table that has drifted by one or a few points through copying, not a post-2021 table; nothing in it is 10 below WIKI.
- If the 2021 change really applied uniformly ("every A-school except diver" per the MyCG commentary quoted by Test Ninjas), then the best available estimate of current values is WIKI minus 10 for every rating except DV (e.g., IT/ET/AET 162, EM 143, GM 199, HS 197, AMT 210, AST 152, DC 145, BM/ME 90, OS/SK/YN/FS 95, PA/IS 99, MST 104, MK 144 or 95), with subtest floors (AR 52, AR 50, MK 56, VE 54/51) unchanged. This is an inference from one anchor (IT) and one secondary quotation, not a verified table, and ME is a counter-signal (the MLEA page still shows 100 in a 2026 capture).
- MIL's "MK Machinery Technician AR + MC + AS = 210" is an outlier against every other source (150/154) and is probably a transcription error (possibly contaminated by the AMT 213/220 row).
- MIL's "AST VE + AR = 162" and "DV VE = 104" are arithmetically implausible for ASVAB standard scores (a two-subtest sum of 162 or a single VE of 104 would require scores far above the normal standard-score ceiling), which indicates the formulas were attached without checking against the numbers.

### Gaps
- No third-party source was found that publishes the post-2021 (162-era) value for any rating other than Test Ninjas quoting MyCG for IT. The official "A School Rate Requirements Nov 2021" file is, per Test Ninjas, on a CAC-protected SharePoint, so no public table exists to settle the remaining ratings.
- The MyCG article itself (https://www.mycg.uscg.mil/News/Article/2816942/asvab-score-requirements-changing-for-a-schools/) returned HTTP 403 / "Access Denied" from this session, so its exact wording on the diver exemption and the uniformity of the 10-point change could not be re-quoted here; the assignment's anchors were used instead.

## Key question 3: Which sources define VE incorrectly or misname subtests (reliability signal)?

### Takeaway
Military.com contradicts itself (body text correct: PC+WK form VE; FAQ wrong: "PC and MK are combined"); OMK and ASVAB Hero's score page use the long-discontinued NO and CS subtests in the YN row; ASVAB Hero's score page invents a weighted VE regression and several four-subtest formulas; JobTestPrep labels a Navy composite table as Coast Guard; HonestMOS lists bare AFQT cutoffs as rating requirements.

### Cited Findings
- Military.com body: "PC and WK are combined to determine a scaled Verbal Expression (VE) score." Military.com FAQ: "Note: PC and MK are combined to create a Verbal Expression score." — [Military.com](https://www.military.com/asvab/asvab-and-coast-guard-jobs).
- Military.com also writes "your actual scores on those two tests must add up to 171" immediately after naming four subtests (AR, MK, EI, GS) — [Military.com](https://www.military.com/asvab/asvab-and-coast-guard-jobs).
- OMK: "YN Yeoman Min. score: 106 and NO+CS of 101"; the page never defines VE, AR, MK, NO or CS — [OMK](https://www.operationmilitarykids.org/asvab-scores-for-coast-guard-jobs/). OMK2 repeats "106 and NO+CS of 101" — [OMK jobs list](https://www.operationmilitarykids.org/coast-guard-jobs-list/).
- HERO-S: "YN ... VE+AR 106 ... NO+CS >= 101"; VE defined as "a single standard score, 0.655×WK + 0.406×PC − 3.03"; and "GT is built from AR + VE and then rescaled, just like the Army calculates it" — [ASVAB Hero score page](https://www.asvabhero.com/coast-guard-asvab-score).
- HERO-S formulas such as "AST VE+AR+MK+MC", "DC VE+AR+MK+MC", "GM VE+AR+MK+MC", "EM VE+AR+MK+EI", "AMT VE+AR+MK+AS", "MK VE+AR+MK+AS", "HS VE+MK" appear on no other source, and its sister page HERO-L gives different formulas for the same ratings (AST VE+MC+AS, DC VE+MC+AS, GM AR+MK+EI+GS, EM MK+EI+GS, AMT AR+MC+AS+EI, MK AR+MC+AS, HS VE+MK+GS) — [ASVAB Hero score page](https://www.asvabhero.com/coast-guard-asvab-score); [ASVAB Hero ratings list](https://www.asvabhero.com/coast-guard-ratings-list).
- HERO-L defines VE correctly: "VE (Verbal Expression) is a combined standard score from Word Knowledge + Paragraph Comprehension; it is not a separate subtest" — but also states "there is no VE, GT, or EL to translate first" two paragraphs earlier, and says the Coast Guard GED minimum was "lowered from 40 in November 2023" while listing it as 50 — [ASVAB Hero ratings list](https://www.asvabhero.com/coast-guard-ratings-list).
- HERO-L lists both "AET Aviation Electrical Technician" and "AVI Avionics Electrical Technician" and claims "the older AVI rating was redesignated as AET" — [ASVAB Hero ratings list](https://www.asvabhero.com/coast-guard-ratings-list). gocoastguard.com's name for AET is "Avionics Electrical Technician (AET)" and it lists no AVI — [gocoastguard.com enlisted careers](https://www.gocoastguard.com/careers/enlisted).
- JobTestPrep: "Verbal Expression (VE) = Word Knowledge (WK) + Paragraph Comprehension (PC)" (correct), but its "Coast Guard" line-score table consists of Navy composites (ENG, ADM, GT, HM, MEC2, EL, NUC, BEE, OPS) — [JobTestPrep](https://www.jobtestprep.com/asvab-coast-guard) (summary only).
- Wikipedia calls VE "not a test, just a combined score" of WK+PC, while its AFQT section says VE comes from a conversion table applied to the combined raw PC and WK scores — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Services_Vocational_Aptitude_Battery).
- Test Ninjas: VE "is the verbal score your sheet derives from Word Knowledge and Paragraph Comprehension"; it also explains AFCT as "the in-service version of the battery" — [Test Ninjas](https://test-ninjas.com/coast-guard-jobs-by-asvab-score).
- HonestMOS gives "AFQT 40" as the requirement for SK, BM, ME, DV, CS and "AFQT 65" for AST, with no composite — [HonestMOS](https://www.honestmos.com/tools/asvab-jobs-by-score) (summary only).
- Peterson's blog never mentions VE; it defines the nine subtests (GS, AR, WK, PC, MK, EI, AS, MC, AO) and AFQT = AR, MK, WK, PC — [Peterson's blog](https://www.petersons.com/blog/coast-guard-asvab-score-requirements-and-how-to-prepare/) (summary only).

### Inferences
- The "NO+CS" artifact (Numerical Operations and Coding Speed, subtests no longer administered in the operational ASVAB) in OMK and HERO-S indicates that lineage's YN row descends from a table compiled decades ago; the same lineage carrying "FS", "PS" and "IV" supports that reading.
- HERO-S's VE regression coefficients are presented without a source and should not be reused; the only defensible statement is that VE is a scaled score derived from WK+PC.
- Military.com's FAQ error (PC+MK) and its two impossible formulas (AST VE+AR=162, DV VE=104) together mean its formulas should not be trusted even where its numbers match other sources.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm from any accessible source when NO and CS were removed from the ASVAB; the statement that they are discontinued rests on general knowledge and is flagged as such.

## Key question 4: Which third-party tables list ratings that are not among gocoastguard.com's 21 enlisted ratings (FS, MU, IV, PS, AVI), or omit ratings that are (CMS, DV, IS)?

### Takeaway
gocoastguard.com lists exactly 21 enlisted ratings (AMT, AST, AET, BM, CS, CMS, DC, DV, EM, ET, GM, HS, IT, IS, MK, MST, ME, OS, PA, SK, YN) and prints no ASVAB score for any. Only Military.com and HonestMOS match that list; OMK adds FS, IV, MU and PS; ASVAB Hero adds PS, IV and a non-existent "AVI" (ratings list) or IV and MU (score page) while omitting CMS; Wikipedia omits CMS, DV and lists FS.

### Cited Findings
- gocoastguard.com /careers/enlisted shows "All results: 21 careers": AMT, AST, AET, BM, CS, CMS, DC, DV, EM, ET, GM, HS, IT, IS, MK, MST, ME, OS, PA, SK, YN; no ASVAB score printed — [gocoastguard.com](https://www.gocoastguard.com/careers/enlisted).
- Military.com lists those same 21 (including CMS and DV) and no others — [Military.com](https://www.military.com/asvab/asvab-and-coast-guard-jobs).
- HonestMOS lists the same 21 (including CMS and DV) — [HonestMOS](https://www.honestmos.com/tools/asvab-jobs-by-score) (summary only).
- OMK lists 24 rows: the 21 minus CMS, plus FS (106), IV (N/A), MU (N/A), PS (101); it lists both "FS Food Service Specialist 106" and "CS Culinary Specialist 106" — [OMK](https://www.operationmilitarykids.org/asvab-scores-for-coast-guard-jobs/). OMK2 lists 22: the 21 minus CMS, plus IV and MU — [OMK jobs list](https://www.operationmilitarykids.org/coast-guard-jobs-list/) (summary only).
- HERO-L lists 22 rows: omits CMS and DV; adds PS, IV and AVI; its prose says "22 enlisted ratings, the fewest of any branch" — [ASVAB Hero ratings list](https://www.asvabhero.com/coast-guard-ratings-list).
- HERO-S lists 22 rows: omits CMS and PS; adds IV and MU; prose says "roughly 22" in one place and "~24" in another — [ASVAB Hero score page](https://www.asvabhero.com/coast-guard-asvab-score).

- Test Ninjas states "The 21 enlisted career pages on gocoastguard.com describe each rating's work and training but print no ASVAB score" — [Test Ninjas](https://test-ninjas.com/coast-guard-jobs-by-asvab-score).

### Inferences
- FS appears to be the legacy name of today's CS rating (Wikipedia's 19-row table has FS and no CS; OMK carries both at the same value), so a table containing FS is older than the CS redesignation; a table containing both FS and CS has been patched rather than rebuilt.
- PS (Port Security Specialist) and IV (Investigator) are not current gocoastguard enlisted careers; their presence (OMK, HERO-L, Peterson's) is another marker of an old underlying list.
- Presence of CMS is the simplest "is this table newer than ~2021" test; only MIL and HMOS pass it, and both fail the MyCG CMS anchor on value/formula.

### Gaps
- I did not locate a dated primary source for the FS-to-CS redesignation or for the PS/IV rating disestablishments; those are stated as inferences.

## Key question 5: Ranking of the third-party sources by apparent reliability, with reasons

### Takeaway
Ranked best to worst for the purpose of a study tool: Test Ninjas (partial but source-attributed and era-aware) > Wikipedia (uncited but a clean, internally consistent pre-2021 table) > ASVAB Hero ratings list (right subtests, wrong era, invented AVI) > Military.com (complete rating list, but impossible formulas and a VE error) > OperationMilitaryKids (no formulas, defunct subtests, obsolete ratings) > ASVAB Hero score page (invented formulas, contradicts its own sister page) > HonestMOS (values match nothing). Peterson's, JobTestPrep, EnlistIQ Prep, MilitarySpot and Study.com publish no usable per-rating table (or could not be verified).

### Cited Findings
- Test Ninjas attributes each figure to a named Coast Guard page and capture month, quotes COMDTINST 1100.2I section 4.B.7 for the 32/47 AFQT floors, and explicitly states the school-page figures may be "10 points high" because of the 1 Nov 2021 change — [Test Ninjas](https://test-ninjas.com/coast-guard-jobs-by-asvab-score). It covers only ET, IT, HS, ME.
- Wikipedia's rows for IT/ET (MK+EI+GS>=172 & AR>=52 or AFQT>=65), HS (VE+MK+GS+AR>=207 & AR>=50) and ME (VE+AR>=100) match the training-command pages Test Ninjas captured, but the table is uncited and flagged by Wikipedia itself — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armed_Services_Vocational_Aptitude_Battery); [Test Ninjas](https://test-ninjas.com/coast-guard-jobs-by-asvab-score).
- ASVAB Hero ratings list uses the three-subtest MK+EI+GS formula for IT/ET/EM/AET (consistent with the IT anchor's subtests), defines VE correctly, cites a review date (Oct 4 2026) and labels rows "provisional"; but it lists a non-existent "AVI" rating, omits CMS and DV, and includes PS and IV — [ASVAB Hero ratings list](https://www.asvabhero.com/coast-guard-ratings-list).
- Military.com is the only full table with the exact gocoastguard 21-rating set including CMS and DV, carries a byline and a date, and defines all subtests; but it gives AST as VE+AR=162 and DV as VE=104 (implausible), MK as 210 (outlier), CMS as AR+MK+EI+GS=171 (contradicts MyCG AR+VE>=110), puts AR inside the IT/ET/AET sum, and its FAQ says VE is PC+MK — [Military.com](https://www.military.com/asvab/asvab-and-coast-guard-jobs).
- OMK gives "Min. score" with no formula for any rating, uses "NO+CS of 101" for YN, lists FS, IV, MU, PS, omits CMS, and cites only "Coast Guard official site" and "Coast Guard recruiting" as references — [OMK](https://www.operationmilitarykids.org/asvab-scores-for-coast-guard-jobs/).
- ASVAB Hero score page contradicts the ratings list on the same site for AST, DC, GM, EM, AMT, MK and HS formulas, asserts an unsourced VE regression, and says the rating count is both "roughly 22" and "~24" — [ASVAB Hero score page](https://www.asvabhero.com/coast-guard-asvab-score).
- HonestMOS cites no primary source and gives values (ET/IT/CMS 222, EM 210, DC/MK 195, MST 209, AMT 209) that appear on no other page checked — [HonestMOS](https://www.honestmos.com/tools/asvab-jobs-by-score) (summary only).
- AFQT enlistment floor as stated by each source (a secondary reliability signal): MIL 32 diploma / 50 GED; HERO-L and HERO-S 32 / 50; Test Ninjas 32 / 47 (COMDTINST 1100.2I); EnlistIQ Prep 32 / 47 (COMDTINST 1100.2I); Peterson's blog 40 / 50; Peterson's what-jobs post "Coast Guard: 32"; JobTestPrep 40 / 50; HonestMOS 40; OMK gives 40 only for the Reserve — sources as cited above ([Military.com](https://www.military.com/asvab/asvab-and-coast-guard-jobs); [ASVAB Hero ratings list](https://www.asvabhero.com/coast-guard-ratings-list); [Test Ninjas](https://test-ninjas.com/coast-guard-jobs-by-asvab-score); [EnlistIQ Prep](https://enlistiqprep.com/asvab-military-guide); [Peterson's blog](https://www.petersons.com/blog/coast-guard-asvab-score-requirements-and-how-to-prepare/); [Peterson's what-jobs](https://www.petersons.com/blog/what-jobs-do-i-qualify-for-with-my-asvab-score-requirements/); [JobTestPrep](https://www.jobtestprep.com/asvab-coast-guard); [HonestMOS](https://www.honestmos.com/tools/asvab-jobs-by-score); [OMK](https://www.operationmilitarykids.org/asvab-scores-for-coast-guard-jobs/)).

### Inferences
- "Most consistent values" across the third-party tables, with the caveat that consistency here mostly means shared copying: ME VE+AR 100 (all six tables); BM VE+AR 101 (four of six; WIKI 100); CS VE+AR 106 (four; WIKI/FS 105); YN VE+AR 106 (four; WIKI/HMOS 105); OS VE+AR 105/106 (split 3-3); SK VE+AR 105/106 (split); PA VE+AR 109/110 (split); MST VE+AR 114/115 (split); GM AR+MK+EI+GS 208/209 (split, formula agreed by four); IT/ET MK+EI+GS (or with AR) 171/172 (five of six; HMOS 222); EM 152/153 (five; HMOS 210); HS 154 (four) vs 207 (WIKI and official school page); AST 162 (five; formula disputed); AMT 213 (four) vs 220 (WIKI) vs 209 (HMOS); DC 152 (four) vs 155 (WIKI) vs 195 (HMOS); MK 150 or VE+AR 106 (three) vs 154/105 (WIKI) vs 210 (MIL) vs 195 (HMOS); IS 100 (MIL, HERO-L) vs 109 (WIKI, HMOS) vs N/A (OMK, HERO-S); DV 104 (three; formula disputed); CMS 171 (MIL) vs 222 (HMOS), both contradicting the MyCG anchor.
- For a study tool, the defensible approach is to display the official anchors (IT, ME, CMS) as the only confirmed values, present WIKI as "pre-Nov-2021 published values" with an explicit note that most A-school composites were lowered by 10 points on 1 Nov 2021 (diver excepted), and treat the MIL/OMK/HERO 171-cluster as unverified third-party figures of pre-2021 vintage.

### Gaps
- Peterson's Test Prep table (?p=171049), Study.com, MilitarySpot and asvabadvantage.com could not be read (browser interstitial, HTTP 403, HTTP 404, DNS/network block respectively), so they are unranked.
- Without Wayback access, the publication-date claims (especially Military.com's "Jul 10, 2026") could not be checked against earlier versions, so no source can be dated by content history; dates above are the sources' own statements.
