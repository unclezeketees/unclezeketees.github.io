# Free, Legitimate ASVAB Practice and Study Resources for a 17-Year-Old in Moravia, NY (Cayuga County) — as of Nov 2026

Research date: 2026-11-09. Scope: free or library-provided resources only; paid upsells flagged. Each claim carries a URL. Where a page could not be read (JavaScript-rendered or blocked), that is recorded as a gap rather than guessed.

## Key Question 1: officialasvab.com sample questions and "Preparing for the ASVAB" advice

### Takeaway
The DoD's official ASVAB site provides free, no-account sample questions for all 10 subtests at ten per-subtest URLs, and its "Preparing for the ASVAB" page explicitly declines to endorse any prep method while naming only three outside options: commercial books at libraries/bookstores, state adult-education programs, and the Army's March2Success.

### Cited Findings
- Index page for sample questions covers all 10 subtests; the user picks a subject and answers its questions. The page does not state question counts or whether answer explanations are given. — [officialasvab.com Sample Questions](https://www.officialasvab.com/applicants/sample-questions/)
- Per-subtest URLs (as linked from the index page):
  - General Science (GS) — https://www.officialasvab.com/general-science-gs/
  - Arithmetic Reasoning (AR) — https://www.officialasvab.com/arithmetic-reasoning-ar/
  - Word Knowledge (WK) — https://www.officialasvab.com/word-knowledge-wk/
  - Paragraph Comprehension (PC) — https://www.officialasvab.com/paragraph-comprehension-pc/
  - Mathematics Knowledge (MK) — https://www.officialasvab.com/mathematics-knowledge-mk/
  - Electronics Information (EI) — https://www.officialasvab.com/electronics-information-ei/
  - Auto Information (AI) — https://www.officialasvab.com/auto-information-ai/
  - Shop Information (SI) — https://www.officialasvab.com/shop-information-si/
  - Mechanical Comprehension (MC) — https://www.officialasvab.com/mechanical-comprehension-mc/
  - Assembling Objects (AO) — https://www.officialasvab.com/assembling-objects-ao/
  — all from [officialasvab.com Sample Questions](https://www.officialasvab.com/applicants/sample-questions/)
- No account or cost: the sample-question pages are open web pages; nothing on the index page mentions registration. — [officialasvab.com Sample Questions](https://www.officialasvab.com/applicants/sample-questions/)
- Applicant-facing "Preparing for the ASVAB" page: the ASVAB Testing Program "does not endorse any particular method of test preparation" and recommends "a solid core of courses in mathematics, English, and science in high school and/or college." It ties academic courses to AR, MK, PC, WK, GS and technical courses to AI, SI, EI, MC. — [Preparing for the ASVAB (applicants)](https://www.officialasvab.com/applicants/preparing-for-the-asvab/)
- Test-day tips on that page: read directions and each question carefully; watch the clock; eliminate wrong choices and make an educated guess; answer easier questions first; answer every question because "there is no penalty for guessing"; review answers if time remains; "Take sample questions and review content areas that need refreshing." — [Preparing for the ASVAB (applicants)](https://www.officialasvab.com/applicants/preparing-for-the-asvab/)
- Recruiter-facing version of the same page (published 2024-05-29) adds that recruiters/testing personnel may not coach applicants or recommend specific products, but may note that "commercially available products are available at local book stores and in public libraries"; "Basic adult education programs are also available through state employment services offices"; and "USD(P&R) has approved an Army-developed online program for basic skills enhancement, called March2Success." It also warns that on the CAT-ASVAB you cannot return to a question and random guesses near the end of a subtest carry "a relatively large penalty." — [Preparing for the ASVAB (recruiters)](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1378)
- Neither page mentions the National Guard practice test or describes the CEP; the CEP appears only as a navigation link. — [Preparing for the ASVAB (applicants)](https://www.officialasvab.com/applicants/preparing-for-the-asvab/); [recruiters version](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1378)

### Inferences
- The official sample questions are the single most authoritative free source of question style, but they are a small familiarization set rather than a full-length practice test; the site itself points users to March2Success for actual skill-building.
- The guessing advice differs by format: "no penalty for guessing" applies to paper-and-pencil; on the computer-adaptive version, finishing with a string of random guesses is penalized, so a student should pace to finish each subtest.

### Gaps
- The exact number of sample questions per subtest and whether explanations are shown were not stated on the index page and the per-subtest pages were not individually fetched.

## Key Question 2: March2Success (march2success.com)

### Takeaway
March2Success is a U.S. Army-sponsored, completely free site requiring individual registration (minimum age 13); its "General Technical Mastery" course gives a diagnostic ASVAB, prescriptive lessons for every ASVAB section, a full-length summative ASVAB, two additional standalone practice exams, and vocabulary/math-science flashcards, with a parent/educator "monitor" feature and an explicit promise that recruiters will not contact users.

### Cited Findings
- Self-description: "your FREE online resource hub" for standardized tests; "proudly sponsored by the US Army" with "no obligation" to connect with the Army; described as a "completely free program." — [march2success.com](https://www.march2success.com/)
- FAQ: "March2Success is provided free of charge to the community with no obligation to the Army." — [March2Success FAQ](https://www.march2success.com/public/faq)
- Recommended ASVAB course: "We recommend using the March2Success for General Technical Mastery course to prepare for the ASVAB." "The course includes assessments, lessons, quizzes and practice tests for each section of the ASVAB." — [March2Success FAQ](https://www.march2success.com/public/faq)
- Detailed course description (National Guard Bureau flyer, created March 2025, hosted by Maryland Military Dept.): students "begin by taking a diagnostic ASVAB test," receive "results for each subject test, as well as their composite scores, including their General Technical score"; the "prescriptive training course provides individual lessons for each learner based on the results of their diagnostic test, featuring engaging multimedia lessons, videos, and gamified exercises"; it ends with "a summative assessment in the form of a full-length ASVAB exam"; "two additional standalone exams are available, as well as flashcard decks for vocabulary and math and science formulas." — [NGB March2Success flyer (PDF)](https://military.maryland.gov/Documents/Tuition%20Assistance%20page/27%20May%20edits%20(flyers)/March2Success.pdf)
- Getting started per the flyer: "first you must register for an account at: https://www.march2success.com. Once you have completed the registration process, you can login and immediately access all course and practice test materials." Demo tour: https://www.march2success.com/demos/showdemo/overview — [NGB March2Success flyer (PDF)](https://military.maryland.gov/Documents/Tuition%20Assistance%20page/27%20May%20edits%20(flyers)/March2Success.pdf)
- Account required: users must "complete a straightforward registration process," select a user type, then log in. — [march2success.com](https://www.march2success.com/)
- Age rule (Terms of Use): "The only requirement to register on March2Success is that you agree to the privacy policy and terms of service" and users must "confirm at registration that you are 13 years old" (COPPA). "March2Success is open to the public." Accounts can be terminated "If We determine you have misrepresented your age." No parental-consent step is described for under-18s. — [March2Success Terms of Use](https://www.march2success.com/public/tou)
- Account lifetime: student and parent accounts are active 180 days from registration; student accounts may be closed after 25 days of inactivity (re-registration presumably possible; not stated). — [March2Success Terms of Use](https://www.march2success.com/public/tou)
- Parent/educator (monitor) feature: "March2Success has a monitor module to allow Educators and Parents to track the progress of their students." "The monitor must provide some information to the students, who will add them as a monitor on their account." Monitors then see basic account info, test results and lesson progress. No separate "mentor" account type is described; bulk school registration is not supported ("The site does not currently support bulk registrations."). — [March2Success FAQ](https://www.march2success.com/public/faq)
- Recruiter contact: Q "Will I be contacted by a recruiter?" A: "No." "March 2 Success does not provide any personal information to recruiters" unless the user is in the Army's Future Soldier Program. Personal data shared with the Army only for Future Soldier Training Program members (or educators who requested program information). — [March2Success FAQ](https://www.march2success.com/public/faq); [march2success.com](https://www.march2success.com/)
- Content partners listed under About Us: MAT, Inc., IMC, Inc., Peterson's, College Options Foundation; content is licensed "through the US Army's agreement with the providers." — [march2success.com](https://www.march2success.com/); [Terms of Use](https://www.march2success.com/public/tou)
- Also offered: SAT/ACT practice tests with percentage and scaled scores, STEM lessons, college-application guidance, and MCAT/DAT/nursing practice tests. — [march2success.com](https://www.march2success.com/)
- Official DoD endorsement-adjacent statement: "USD(P&R) has approved an Army-developed online program for basic skills enhancement, called March2Success." — [officialasvab.com Preparing for the ASVAB (recruiters)](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1378)
- Also listed as prep material by the New York National Guard's Education Services office (see Key Question 6). — [NY DMNA AFCT page](https://dmna.ny.gov/education/?id=afct)

### Inferences
- For a 17-year-old, March2Success is the best free full-length ASVAB practice option: it is the only free source identified that offers multiple full-length ASVAB exams plus adaptive lessons, with no ads and no recruiter follow-up. A parent can be added as a "monitor" after the student registers.
- The 180-day account window means a student should register close to when they intend to study, or expect to re-register.

### Gaps
- Whether the three practice exams are timed/computer-adaptive in format, and exact item counts, were not stated on the pages read.
- The FAQ's exact wording on Future Soldier data sharing was paraphrased by the fetch tool; the sense (no sharing unless enrolled in Future Soldier program) is clear but the full sentence was not captured verbatim.

## Key Question 3: ASVAB Career Exploration Program (CEP) via high school

### Takeaway
The DoD-sponsored ASVAB CEP is free to schools and students ("No Cost. No Commitment."), is taken at a participating high school in grades 10-12, gives ASVAB scores plus two years of access to career-planning tools at asvabprogram.com, and 11th/12th-grade CEP scores are valid for enlistment for two years after the test date regardless of which of the eight recruiter-release options the school chooses (Option 1 = release 7 days after scores mailed; Option 2 = 60 days; Option 8 = never released, yet still valid for enlistment).

### Cited Findings
- Sponsor and mission: "The Department of Defense sponsors the ASVAB CEP with a two-part mission: to provide a career exploration service to American youth and provide qualified leads to Military recruiters." "Participants have no obligation to talk to a Military recruiter or enter Military service. However, students in 11th grade and beyond can use their scores to consider enlistment." — [ASVAB CEP Fact Sheet (PDF)](https://prod-media.asvabprogram.com/CEP_PDF_Contents/fact_sheet.pdf)
- Cost: "No Cost. No Commitment." Toolkit is "free to share." "Test administration and Post-Test Interpretations - we do all the work so schools don't have to!" — [ASVAB CEP Fact Sheet (PDF)](https://prod-media.asvabprogram.com/CEP_PDF_Contents/fact_sheet.pdf)
- Grades/format: "All students in 10th grade and above are encouraged to participate. Participation in the ASVAB CEP is ALWAYS voluntary. The test is available in two formats, the paper and pencil or computer-adaptive (iCAT)." — [ASVAB CEP Fact Sheet (PDF)](https://prod-media.asvabprogram.com/CEP_PDF_Contents/fact_sheet.pdf)
- Ninth graders may not take it: "Federal law does not allow ninth graders to take the test." Grade 10 results "do not count for military recruiting purposes." — [California Dept. of Education ASVAB FAQ](https://www.cde.ca.gov/Ds/sp/cl/asvabtestfaq.asp)
- Components: ASVAB test; Post-Test Interpretation session; Find Your Interests (FYI) inventory "Based on John Holland's widely accepted theory of career choice"; Work Values: Situational Judgment Activity; OCCU-Find ("a database containing relevant, reliable occupational data"); Career Plans; Portfolio (resume builder); Calendar; Classroom Activities; Careersinthemilitary.com (shows "Service-specific ASVAB line scores"). — [ASVAB CEP Fact Sheet (PDF)](https://prod-media.asvabprogram.com/CEP_PDF_Contents/fact_sheet.pdf)
- Results/access period: "Score sheets include an access code that allow students to use the resources available at asvabprogram.com for two years." — [ASVAB CEP Admin Overview (PDF, Oct 2025)](https://prod-media.asvabprogram.com/CEP_PDF_Contents/ASVAB_CEP_Admin_Overview_web.pdf)
- Enlistment validity: "Regardless of the release option selected by your school, 11th and 12th grade student ASVAB CEP scores are valid for enlistment for two years after the test date. Students interested in military service can contact a recruiter on their own." — [ASVAB CEP Admin Overview (PDF)](https://prod-media.asvabprogram.com/CEP_PDF_Contents/ASVAB_CEP_Admin_Overview_web.pdf)
- Release options ("Your school chooses from eight score release options"): Option 1 — "7 days after test scores are mailed."; Option 2 — "60 days after test scores are mailed. No contact prior to that time."; Option 3 — 90 days; Option 4 — 120 days; Option 5 — "End of school year. No contact prior to that time."; Option 6 — "7 days after test scores are mailed. No telephone solicitations by recruiters."; Option 7 — "Administrative option used by USMEPCOM ONLY for test administration issues ... Not valid for enlistment purposes. Results not released"; Option 8 — "Not released to the Services (While student results are not released to the Services, scores are valid for enlistment for two years after the test date.)" — [ASVAB CEP Admin Overview (PDF)](https://prod-media.asvabprogram.com/CEP_PDF_Contents/ASVAB_CEP_Admin_Overview_web.pdf)
- Split option: "Schools may select a 'split option,' where two release options are used: one option allows release of scores to recruiters and one option restricts that release to accommodate individual requests." — [ASVAB CEP Admin Overview (PDF)](https://prod-media.asvabprogram.com/CEP_PDF_Contents/ASVAB_CEP_Admin_Overview_web.pdf)
- Parent opt in/out: "Regardless of the score release option your school selects, parents can choose to opt in or out of having their child's scores released. Forms can be found at asvabprogram.com/general-resources." — [ASVAB CEP Admin Overview (PDF)](https://prod-media.asvabprogram.com/CEP_PDF_Contents/ASVAB_CEP_Admin_Overview_web.pdf)
- Recruiter contact caveat: "Your students may be contacted by college and military recruiters regardless of ASVAB CEP participation or the score release option your school chooses" (because federally funded schools must provide directory information). — [ASVAB CEP Admin Overview (PDF)](https://prod-media.asvabprogram.com/CEP_PDF_Contents/ASVAB_CEP_Admin_Overview_web.pdf)
- How a school participates: "Your local ASVAB CEP Education Services Specialist (ESS) is your communication point of contact"; "Schools offering the ASVAB CEP must provide a facility for testing"; test sessions "are administered by DoD civilians but may be supported by a military personnel proctor (one proctor for every 40 students)"; schools choose paper or iCAT format. Educators can preview tools with access code CEP4ME. — [ASVAB CEP Admin Overview (PDF)](https://prod-media.asvabprogram.com/CEP_PDF_Contents/ASVAB_CEP_Admin_Overview_web.pdf)
- "The CEP is only available to students who take the ASVAB at their high school." Students who opt out of recruiter contact "will still receive their ASVAB results and will have access to the Career Exploratory Program." — [California Dept. of Education ASVAB FAQ](https://www.cde.ca.gov/Ds/sp/cl/asvabtestfaq.asp)
- Scale: "+3 Million in 5 Years" participants. — [ASVAB CEP Fact Sheet (PDF)](https://prod-media.asvabprogram.com/CEP_PDF_Contents/fact_sheet.pdf)
- Public program site (JavaScript-rendered; body not readable by fetch tool): https://www.asvabprogram.com/ ; general resources/opt-out forms: https://www.asvabprogram.com/general-resources — [asvabprogram.com](https://www.asvabprogram.com/)
- New York State Education Department maintains a page pointing schools to the ASVAB CEP as a free career-planning resource. — [NYSED ASVAB Career Exploration Program](https://nysed.gov/standards-instruction/asvab-career-exploration-program) (page returned 503 when fetched; existence confirmed via search result)
- Moravia Central School District: no ASVAB/CEP information found on its site. The district's High School Counseling page lists Shannon Taylor, High School Counselor, and Sarah Crossgrove, Secretary. Moravia Jr-Sr High School: 68 S Main St, Moravia, NY 13118, (315) 497-2670. — [Moravia CSD counseling page](https://www.moraviaschool.org/Page/51); [ProPublica school listing](https://projects.propublica.org/miseducation/school/361980001813)

### Inferences
- A 17-year-old junior or senior who takes the CEP at school gets a real ASVAB (paper or computer-adaptive) for free, and the resulting AFQT/line scores can be handed to a Coast Guard recruiter for up to two years even if the school chose Option 8 (no release), so the CEP doubles as a free "official practice run" that may also count as the real thing.
- To find out whether Moravia participates, the practical route is to ask the high school counselor (Shannon Taylor) or call the school; there is no public lookup of participating schools on the pages reviewed.

### Gaps
- asvabprogram.com pages (home, /students, /faq) are JavaScript-rendered and returned no body text; details above come from the program's own official PDFs instead.
- NYSED's CEP page returned HTTP 503; its content (e.g., NY ESS contact names) could not be read.
- Could not confirm whether Moravia Central School District currently offers the ASVAB CEP or what release option it uses.
- The minimum AFQT for Coast Guard enlistment is outside this note's scope and gocoastguard.com's FAQ/eligibility pages did not state it in the content fetched (see Key Question 5).

## Key Question 4: Library-provided test prep (NOVELny, FLLS, Cayuga County libraries, Powers Library; DoD MWR Libraries)

### Takeaway
NOVELny — the New York State Library's statewide database program — now includes "Gale Presents: Peterson's Test and Career Prep" (eBooks, online courses, full-length practice tests), which reaches Powers Library patrons through the Finger Lakes Library System's online-databases page ("Testing & Careers" category) with a library barcode; Powers Library's own website lists only Libby, Mango and TumbleBooks, so the student should go through the FLLS databases page or ask staff. DoD MWR Libraries are free but only for service members and their families (DEERS-authenticated), so they are not an option unless a parent is military.

### Cited Findings
- NOVELny definition: "Provided by the New York State Library, NOVELny is an online virtual library connecting New Yorkers to 21st century information via libraries and library systems statewide." "NOVELny is supported primarily with New York State funds" plus federal LSTA/IMLS funds. — [NOVELny Factsheet (PDF hosted by FLLS, May 2025 upload; metadata dated Oct 2024)](https://www.flls.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Revised_NOVELnyFactsheet.pdf)
- NOVELny includes "Gale Presents: Peterson's Test and Career Prep — Prepare for standardized tests with eBooks, online courses, and full-length practice tests for GED, SAT, ACT, AP, PSAT, GRE, LSAT, MCAT, TOEFL, U.S. citizenship, and more. Research undergraduate and graduate programs, find tuition assistance, and explore and prepare for careers ... Search for jobs and create résumés, cover letters, and websites using prebuilt templates." Other NOVELny products: Britannica School/Academic/Escolar, Gale Academic OneFile Select, Gale OneFile: High School Edition, Gale In Context (Middle School, Opposing Viewpoints, Elementary), Gale Health and Wellness, Gale Legal Forms (NYS), Gale Books and Authors, Gale Business products, Gale OneFile: News, etc. — [NOVELny Factsheet (PDF)](https://www.flls.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Revised_NOVELnyFactsheet.pdf)

## Key Question 5: Other free options — Khan Academy, Union Test Prep, 4Tests, Coast Guard recommendations (ranked by legitimacy)

### Takeaway
Khan Academy is a free, ad-free 501(c)(3) nonprofit that has no ASVAB-specific course but covers the arithmetic/algebra/geometry underlying AR and MK; Union Test Prep and 4Tests are free commercial sites (ad- or upsell-supported) that the New York National Guard itself lists as prep links, with 4Tests offering a 135-question free practice set and Union Test Prep about 350 free questions plus flashcards and a paid "cram course"; the Coast Guard's recruiting site publishes no ASVAB study recommendations in the pages fetched.

### Cited Findings

## Key Question 6: New York State programs (NY National Guard / DMNA) offering March2Success or ASVAB prep
