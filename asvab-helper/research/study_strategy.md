# ASVAB Test-Taking Strategy and Subtest-by-Subtest Study Guide (CAT-ASVAB at MEPS, Coast Guard applicant, 2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-11-09. Scope: official preparation guidance, CAT-ASVAB pacing/guessing strategy, study timeline, per-subtest topics and free review sources, Coast Guard subtest emphasis, test-day logistics. Official sources = officialasvab.com, asvabprogram.com, uscg.mil/mycg.uscg.mil. Everything else is labeled third party. Several target pages (Kaplan, Union Test Prep, All About Circuits, CK-12, Khan Academy unit pages, mycg.uscg.mil) returned 403/bot-check pages during this research and were not bypassed; they are recorded in Gaps.

## Key Question 1: What does the official ASVAB program say about preparing (what works, what does not)?

### Takeaway
The official program does not endorse any prep method or product; it says the best preparation is a solid core of high school math, English and science (academic courses for AR/MK/PC/WK/GS; technical courses for AI/SI/EI/MC), plus becoming familiar with each subtest, working the official sample questions, and resting the night before. Official test-taking advice: read directions and questions carefully, watch the clock, eliminate wrong choices and make an educated guess, and on the CAT-ASVAB answer every item legitimately rather than random-guessing a string at the end.

### Cited Findings
- "The ASVAB Testing Program does not endorse any particular method of test preparation beyond recommending that examinees take a solid core of courses in mathematics, English, and science in high school and/or college." Academic courses help AR, MK, PC, WK, GS; technical courses help AI, SI, EI, MC. — [officialasvab.com, Preparing for the ASVAB (?p=1384)](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1384)
- Official pre-test steps: prepare well in advance; learn what to expect on test day; become familiar with the content of each subtest; take the sample questions and review content areas that need refreshing; get plenty of rest the night before. — [officialasvab.com, Preparing for the ASVAB](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1384)
- Official test-taking strategies: read each test's directions carefully before starting; read each question carefully; watch the time and do not spend so long on one question that you run out of time; if you don't know, rule out as many wrong choices as you can and make an educated guess; answer every question (on paper, "there is no penalty for guessing"); select only one response. — [officialasvab.com, Preparing for the ASVAB](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1384)
- Separate official CAT-ASVAB guidance: learn how the CAT-ASVAB works before test day; you can review the instructions as often as needed during the session (time in instructions/HELP does not count against the subtest clock per the official CAT-ASVAB materials summarized in search results); "you aren't allowed to return to a question once you have answered it," so confirm your choice before submitting; "the CAT-ASVAB applies a relatively large penalty when several incorrect answers are provided toward the end of a subtest," so when time runs short keep answering legitimately rather than guessing at random. — [officialasvab.com (?p=1378)](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1378); [officialasvab.com (?p=1142)](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1142)
- The official site states DoD/Service personnel are prohibited from buying, maintaining or recommending any specific test-preparation product (to avoid implied endorsement), and lists an "ASVAB Test Preparation Disclaimer" (official statement regarding fraudulent ASVAB test-preparation claims) in its Applicants menu. — [officialasvab.com Applicants index (?p=1023)](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1023); search summary of officialasvab.com preparation pages ([search result](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1384))
- Official sample questions exist for all 10 subtests ("actual questions taken from retired ASVAB forms"), with these official content descriptions: GS = physical and biological sciences; AR = basic arithmetic word problems; WK = pick the synonym that best matches a given word; PC = obtain information from written passages; MK = high-school math principles; EI = electricity and electronics; AI = automobile technology; SI = tools and shop terminology/practices; MC = mechanical and physical principles; AO = how an object looks when its parts are put together. — [officialasvab.com Sample Questions](https://www.officialasvab.com/applicants/sample-questions/); [Applicants index](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1023)
- Student (CEP) sample items are also provided at asvabprogram.com/student and in the CEP Overview; counselors are told to tell students "to take the test seriously." — [ASVAB CEP Counselor Manual (PDF), p.3-4](https://prod-media.asvabprogram.com/CEP_PDF_Contents/ASVAB_CEP_Counselor_Manual.pdf)
- Counselor Manual's description of how the adaptive test works: "All examinees begin the test with a question of average difficulty. If the examinee answers the first question correctly, the computer presents a harder item for question two. If the examinee answers question two incorrectly, the computer presents an easier item for question three," i.e., it "presents the best question for the individual." — [ASVAB CEP Counselor Manual, p.5](https://prod-media.asvabprogram.com/CEP_PDF_Contents/ASVAB_CEP_Counselor_Manual.pdf)
- Official "Myths" page: neither CAT nor paper version is easier; the CAT can "feel" harder because items are tailored to your ability; scores are statistically linked (equated) so you should get a similar score on either; a calculator would not help because scores would be adjusted to offset it. — [officialasvab.com Myths (?p=1339)](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1339)
- Official FAQ: the ASVAB is an employment test, not an achievement test, so no calculator; "Knowledge of basic math and the ability to do hand calculations (without a calculator) may be required on the job." — [officialasvab.com FAQs](https://www.officialasvab.com/faqs/)
- Military.com (third party) and DANTES warn about test-prep companies selling questionable products, multi-test bundles paid up front, and "guaranteed score" claims. — [Military.com, Avoid test prep scams](https://www.military.com/education/timesaving-programs/avoid-test-prep-scams.html)

### Inferences
- The official position effectively says: content review (math, vocabulary, reading, science) + familiarity with format = what works; no official source claims any trick, product or cram method raises scores. A study tool can safely present "review the content you are weak in, practice the real format, rest" as the officially endorsed approach and present everything else as third-party advice.
- The Counselor Manual's "Preparing for Test Administration" section is about school logistics (rooms, proctors, 3-hour block), not student coaching; it contains no student-prep advice beyond "take it seriously."

### Gaps
- The official "ASVAB Test Preparation Disclaimer" page text could not be retrieved (only its menu title appears); its exact wording on fraudulent claims is unknown.
- The counselor-facing page officialasvab.com/prepare_coun.htm was not fetched.

## Key Question 2: CAT-ASVAB strategy: pacing, guessing, no going back, time running out, adaptive difficulty

### Takeaway
Official rules: 135 scored items across 10 subtests in 197 minutes (longer if unscored tryout items are seeded); answers lock and cannot be revisited; if you run out of time the unanswered items are "scored as though they were answered at random," and "several incorrect answers toward the end of a subtest" carry "a relatively large penalty." The official site says time limits are generous enough that the penalty is almost never needed, so the practical strategy is: keep a steady pace using the on-screen timer, never leave a subtest unfinished, but never dump random answers at the end; early items move the ability estimate most but every answer counts.

### Cited Findings
- Official CAT-ASVAB table (scored items / possible tryout items / minutes without tryouts / minutes with tryouts): GS 15/15/12/25; AR 15/15/55/113; WK 15/15/9/18; PC 10/15/27/75; MK 15/15/31/65; EI 15/15/10/21; AI 10/15/7/18; SI 10/15/6/17; MC 15/15/22/42; AO 15/15/18/38. Totals (computed from table, not stated by page): 135 scored items, 197 minutes without tryouts, 432 minutes with tryouts. — [officialasvab.com, The CAT-ASVAB](https://www.officialasvab.com/applicants/cat-asvab/); same table at [?p=1127](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1127)
- Adaptive mechanics (official): items are selected from a pool so that "the item best suited for the examinee's estimated ability level is selected to be administered next"; correct answer -> harder item next, incorrect -> easier. The CAT provides "higher levels of test-score precision and shorter test lengths than the P&P-ASVAB." — [officialasvab.com, The CAT-ASVAB](https://www.officialasvab.com/applicants/cat-asvab/)
- Penalty for not finishing (official): "A penalty procedure is applied to all examinees who do not complete the test before time runs out. This is done by scoring the items that were not completed as though they were answered at random." "In almost all cases it is not necessary to apply the penalty" because time limits are generous enough that nearly everyone finishes. — [officialasvab.com (?p=1127)](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1127); [applicants/cat-asvab](https://www.officialasvab.com/applicants/cat-asvab/)
- Penalty for end-of-subtest random guessing (official): "the CAT-ASVAB applies a relatively large penalty when several incorrect answers are provided toward the end of a subtest"; random guessing near the end is discouraged; when time is short, read and answer the remaining questions properly. — [officialasvab.com (?p=1378)](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1378); [officialasvab.com (?p=1142)](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1142)
- No returning to items (official): "you aren't allowed to return to a question once you have answered it," so double-check before confirming. — [officialasvab.com (?p=1142)](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1142)
- Test flow (official "What to Expect"): on the CAT you work at your own pace and may move to the next subtest when finished; the remaining time and number of questions appear in the lower right-hand corner of the screen; the whole computer session takes about 2 hours; scores are available immediately after. — [officialasvab.com, What to Expect](https://www.officialasvab.com/applicants/what-to-expect/)
- Official Fact Sheet (as summarized in search results): most examinees finish before the time limit; if time is running short, keep answering as well as you can rather than guessing randomly because the CAT-ASVAB has a penalty; on the paper version there is no penalty for guessing. — [ASVAB Fact Sheet copy hosted by Sheboygan schools (PDF)](https://www.sheboygan.k12.wi.us/perch/resources/asvabfactsheet-1.pdf) (direct fetch returned 404; content taken from search snippet)
- Official statement that neither format is harder; scores are equated across CAT and paper forms. — [officialasvab.com Myths](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1339); [FAQs](https://www.officialasvab.com/faqs/)
- Third party (Dummies/Wiley): on the CAT, "harder questions are worth more points than easier questions"; the first several answers set the difficulty path, so answer them accurately; you cannot skip or change answers; set a rough time limit per question; commit to each answer and move on; double-check before submitting; when guessing on several items in a row pick the same letter; do not change answers unless you are sure you misread (cites an Air Force study where changed answers went right-to-wrong over 72% of the time). — [Dummies, Pros and cons of the CAT-ASVAB](https://www.dummies.com/article/academics-the-arts/study-skills-test-prep/armed-services/pros-and-cons-of-the-computerized-asvab-cat-asvab-150380); [Dummies, Guessing smart](https://www.dummies.com/article/academics-the-arts/study-skills-test-prep/armed-services/what-to-do-when-you-dont-know-an-answer-on-the-asvab-guessing-smart-142393)
- Third party (Dummies): elimination tactics: cross out clearly wrong choices to get to two; absolute words ("always," "never," "none") usually mark wrong answers; if two choices mean the same thing both are probably wrong; if two contradict, one is usually right; each question is independent of the previous answer letter. — [Dummies, Guessing smart](https://www.dummies.com/article/academics-the-arts/study-skills-test-prep/armed-services/what-to-do-when-you-dont-know-an-answer-on-the-asvab-guessing-smart-142393)
- Third party (Test-Ninjas, which cites ASVAB Technical Bulletin No. 3 and officialasvab.com): per-item budget = time limit / scored items: GS ~48 s; AR ~3 min 40 s; WK ~36 s; PC ~2 min 42 s; MK ~2 min 4 s; EI ~40 s; AI ~42 s; SI ~36 s; MC ~1 min 28 s; AO ~1 min 12 s. Early answers move the ability estimate fastest "but every answer counts in the final score"; the claim that "the first five questions decide your score" is wrong because the final score is re-estimated from every response; the unfinished-item penalty grows with the number of unfinished items. Glance at the on-screen counter once or twice per subtest. — [Test-Ninjas, How the CAT-ASVAB works](https://test-ninjas.com/how-the-cat-asvab-works)
- Third party (Practice Test Geeks, commercial) advises slowing down on the first five items of each subtest because the algorithm calibrates on early answers — [Practice Test Geeks](https://practicetestgeeks.com/asvab/exam-tips); contradicted in emphasis by [Test-Ninjas](https://test-ninjas.com/how-the-cat-asvab-works), which says every answer counts.
- Third party (Mometrix): once you submit you cannot review or change answers, so be deliberate; the whole CAT is 197 minutes; focus study on math and verbal since AFQT is built from them. — [Mometrix ASVAB page](https://www.mometrix.com/academy/asvab-test/)
- Third party (Kaplan, from search snippet only; page returned 403): "your performance on one question directly influences the difficulty of the next," so the CAT needs format-specific strategy; math strategies include backsolving (plug answer choices into word problems) and picking numbers (substitute simple values for variables); predict the answer before looking at the choices. — [Kaplan, General strategies for the ASVAB](https://www.kaptest.com/study/asvab/asvab-strategies/)

### Inferences
- Combining the official statements: the only ways to lose points beyond simply answering wrong are (a) leaving items unanswered (scored as random) and (b) a cluster of wrong answers at the end (interpreted by the scoring as rapid guessing). Therefore the best policy for a tool to teach is "finish every subtest with real attempts; if you must guess, guess after elimination, and spread the fast decisions across the subtest rather than saving them for the last few items."
- WK (36 s/item), SI (36 s/item), EI (40 s/item), AI (42 s/item) and GS (48 s/item) are knowledge-recall tests where you either know it or don't; a tool should tell the student to answer these quickly and not agonize. AR (3 min 40 s/item), PC (2 min 42 s/item) and MK (2 min 4 s/item) have generous time, so careful reading and double-checking arithmetic pays off there.
- Because tryout (unscored) items may appear and extend time limits, a student should not panic if a subtest shows more items/time than expected; the on-screen counter is the authority.

- The exact penalty formula (Technical Bulletin No. 3) was not read directly; only Test-Ninjas' description of it was available.

## Key Question 3: Study timeline, diagnostic-first approach, use of practice tests, realistic AFQT improvement

### Takeaway
No official timeline exists; third-party guides converge on 4-8 weeks of 45-90 minutes a day, starting with a timed full-length diagnostic, prioritizing the weakest AFQT subtests (AR, MK first), retaking a practice test every ~2 weeks, and tapering the last day. No credible source gives a measured average AFQT gain from studying; the only hard numbers are an Army prep-program average of +17 points (score type unclear) and the official rule that a gain of 20+ AFQT points within six months triggers a confirmation test.

### Cited Findings
- Dummies/Wiley schedule (third party): one month out, take a full ASVAB practice exam and score each subtest; rank the four AFQT subtests 1-4 and the specialty subtests 1-5; spend the most time on the lowest-ranked, "especially Mathematics Knowledge and Arithmetic Reasoning"; two weeks out, retake the same practice exam and compare, write down specific weaknesses, drop subjects where you missed only 1-2 items; take another practice test periodically; the day before, two one-hour review sessions, pack required items, set an alarm, hydrate, at least eight hours of sleep. — [Dummies, Following an ASVAB study schedule](https://www.dummies.com/article/academics-the-arts/study-skills-test-prep/armed-services/following-an-asvab-study-schedule-164183)
- Test-Ninjas 4-week plan (third party): 60-70 minutes a day, 6 days a week; Day 1 timed baseline on the four AFQT subtests; week 1 fundamentals, week 2 word problems, week 3 algebra/geometry, week 4 timed mixed practice; from week 3 run at least one block daily under real time limits; one full timed practice test early in week 4 then work its weak areas; last 2 days light; if math is far behind verbal, cut vocabulary to 15 min and give math 40 min. It states "no one can promise a specific number" of points. Illustrative arithmetic (not a forecast): from a 45-45-45-45 standard-score profile, +5 on WK and PC moves the AFQT percentile from 28th to 38th; +5 on AR and MK to 38th; WK is worth about 1.3 AFQT-composite points per standard-score point, AR and MK about 1 each, PC about 0.8. — [Test-Ninjas, How to improve AFQT score](https://test-ninjas.com/how-to-improve-afqt-score)
- Aggregated third-party timeline claims (search summary, mostly commercial sites, not standardized): 4-8 weeks at 6-10 hours/week; beginners 8-12 weeks; 60-90 minutes a day 5-6 days a week; totals of 40-80 hours over 4-6 weeks, or 30-40 hours for a strong academic background up to 80-100+ hours for significant gaps; more than ~15 hours/week risks burnout; spaced sessions beat one long block. — e.g., [Peterson's ASVAB test prep](https://www.petersons.com/asvab); [Military Prep Hub study schedule](https://www.militaryprephub.com/blog/asvab-study-schedule.html); [JobCannon 4-week plan](https://jobcannon.io/blog/asvab-study-plan-four-weeks) (none fetched directly; snippets only)
- Mometrix (third party): use multiple practice tests; focus on math and verbal because AFQT comes from them. — [Mometrix](https://www.mometrix.com/academy/asvab-test/)
- Official retest policy: one-month wait after the initial test; one month before a second retest; six months thereafter; the same policy applies whether the first test was a student or enlistment test; an AFQT gain of 20+ points within six months requires a confirmation test (C-Test). Scores may be used for enlistment for up to two years. — [officialasvab.com Retest Policy (?p=1343)](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1343); [officialasvab.com FAQs](https://www.officialasvab.com/faqs/)
- Army (official, .mil): students in the Army's expanded academic prep program (Future Soldier Preparatory Course) on average increased "their academic scores by 17 points" (the article does not clearly specify AFQT vs another score). — [Army.mil](https://www.army.mil/article-amp/265637/expanded_academic_program_offers_additional_benefits_for_initial_recruits)
- Navy (unofficial site NavyCS): a retest that increases 20 or more AFQT points over the previous score requires a confirmation test. — [NavyCS Confirmation Test](https://www.navycs.com/meps/confirm-asvab.html)
- A commercial blog claim that "10-20+ points on the AFQT are achievable in a one-month focused study window" cites no data (search summary; label as unsupported marketing). — [Air Force Hugo blog](https://airforce-hugo.pages.dev/blog/how-to-raise-your-asvab-line-scores)
- DTIC research exists on practice/retest effects (ADA148314, "effects of massed retesting or practice on ASVAB subtest and composite scores"; ADA216122, test-retest data on 4,000+ subjects) but full texts were not readable here (503). — [DTIC ADA148314](https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/pdfs/ADA148314.pdf); [DTIC ADA216122](https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/pdfs/ADA216122.pdf)
- An OMB/DoD information-collection document describes a planned Army National Guard randomized study of whether giving applicants ASVAB prep books raises scores or retest gains; results not located. — [OMB.report](https://omb.report/icr/200808-0704-001/doc/8200601)
- Free practice-test sources (third party, not fetched; status from search): official sample questions for all 10 subtests at [officialasvab.com](https://www.officialasvab.com/applicants/sample-questions/) and student samples at [asvabprogram.com/sample-questions](https://www.asvabprogram.com/sample-questions); free full practice sets at Mometrix GS (free test; course is paid, 20% code advertised) [Mometrix](https://www.mometrix.com/academy/asvab-general-science-practice-test); Easy-Prep (70 AO questions, "completely free, no sign-up") [easy-prep.org](https://easy-prep.org/asvab-practice-test/assembling-objects-practice-test); HonestMOS free practice tool [honestmos.com](https://www.honestmos.com/tools/asvab-practice); Test-Guide AO practice [test-guide.com](https://www.test-guide.com/asvab/free-asvab-practice-test/asvab-assembling-objects-practice-test-1.html).

### Inferences
- For a 17-year-old with an enlistment target, a defensible plan is: timed diagnostic on day 1 -> 4-8 weeks, ~1 hour/day, 5-6 days/week -> math first (AR/MK are each worth ~1 AFQT-composite point per standard-score point and respond well to review), WK second (worth ~1.3 per point, but vocabulary gains are slow), then the technical subtests the target Coast Guard rating needs -> a full timed practice test every 2 weeks -> light review and 8 hours sleep the night before.
- "Realistic improvement" cannot be quantified from credible data. A tool should say: gains depend on starting point and hours; large gains (20+ AFQT points within 6 months) are flagged officially for a confirmation test, which implies such jumps are considered unusual.
- Because the first official score is usable for two years and retests have waiting periods, a student should treat the first MEPS attempt as the real thing rather than a diagnostic; use free practice tests for the diagnostic instead.

- No peer-reviewed or official statistic on average AFQT gain from self-study was found; DTIC reports were unreadable (HTTP 503).
- Magoosh and Peterson's "how to study" pages returned 404 at the URLs tried; their specific timelines are not captured.
- Union Test Prep's "How do I practice for the ASVAB" page returned 403.

## Key Question 4: Subtest by subtest: what to review and the best free place to review it

### Takeaway
Official content descriptions are brief, so topic lists below combine the official one-line descriptions with third-party prep outlines (labeled). Free review sources with verified-reachable URLs: Khan Academy top-level courses (pages served a bot-check so unit lists could not be confirmed, see Gaps), OpenStax textbooks (all URLs below returned HTTP 200), official sample questions, Dummies/Wiley articles, Test-Ninjas, Mometrix free practice tests. All About Circuits, CK-12 and Union Test Prep returned 403 to automated fetches and are listed as well-known free sources but unverified in this research.

### Cited Findings

#### Arithmetic Reasoning (AR) - 15 items, 55 min (~3 min 40 s per item)
- Official content: "solving basic arithmetic word problems." — [officialasvab.com Sample Questions](https://www.officialasvab.com/applicants/sample-questions/); Counselor Manual: "Measuring ability to solve basic arithmetic word problems" — [CEP Counselor Manual p.4](https://prod-media.asvabprogram.com/CEP_PDF_Contents/ASVAB_CEP_Counselor_Manual.pdf)
- Third-party method (Mometrix): identify what the question asks, write it as an equation, list the information needed, list the steps, practice until automatic. — [Mometrix](https://www.mometrix.com/academy/asvab-test/)
- Third-party method (Kaplan, snippet): backsolving (plug the answer choices back into the word problem) and picking numbers. — [Kaplan strategies](https://www.kaptest.com/study/asvab/asvab-strategies/)
- Third-party topic weighting (Test-Ninjas 4-week plan): week 2 is entirely word problems (rates, ratios, percents, work/mixture), week 3 algebra and geometry. — [Test-Ninjas](https://test-ninjas.com/how-to-improve-afqt-score)
- Free review (Khan Academy, top-level course URLs; unit pages not verified, see Gaps): Arithmetic [https://www.khanacademy.org/math/arithmetic](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/arithmetic); Pre-algebra [https://www.khanacademy.org/math/pre-algebra](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/pre-algebra) (units include fractions, decimals, negative numbers, ratios & rates, percents, exponents & radicals, equations & expressions, measurement; likely unit slugs e.g. /math/pre-algebra/pre-algebra-fractions, /pre-algebra-ratios-rates, /pre-algebra-percents, /pre-algebra-exponents-radicals, /pre-algebra-equations-expressions, /pre-algebra-measurement - all responded HTTP 200 but Khan serves 200 to any path, so treat slugs as unverified); Get ready for Algebra 1 [https://www.khanacademy.org/math/get-ready-for-algebra-i](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/get-ready-for-algebra-i); probability basics in 7th-grade statistics & probability [https://www.khanacademy.org/math/cc-seventh-grade-math/cc-7th-probability-statistics](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/cc-seventh-grade-math/cc-7th-probability-statistics).
- No calculator is allowed (official), so practice hand arithmetic. — [officialasvab.com FAQs](https://www.officialasvab.com/faqs/); [What to Expect](https://www.officialasvab.com/applicants/what-to-expect/)

#### Mathematics Knowledge (MK) - 15 items, 31 min (~2 min per item)
- Official content: "knowledge of high school math principles" / "mathematical concepts and applications." — [officialasvab.com Sample Questions](https://www.officialasvab.com/applicants/sample-questions/); [CEP Counselor Manual p.4](https://prod-media.asvabprogram.com/CEP_PDF_Contents/ASVAB_CEP_Counselor_Manual.pdf)
- Third-party topic list (Mometrix): order of operations, exponents, simple geometry (circles, triangles, rectangles), fractions, percentages and interest, factorials, number types (real, integer, rational, prime). — [Mometrix](https://www.mometrix.com/academy/asvab-test/)
- Free review (Khan Academy top-level courses): Algebra 1 [https://www.khanacademy.org/math/algebra](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/algebra) (solving equations & inequalities, linear equations & graphs, systems, exponents & radicals, quadratics/factoring); Basic geometry and measurement [https://www.khanacademy.org/math/basic-geo](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/basic-geo) (area & perimeter, angles, Pythagorean theorem, volume & surface area); Pre-algebra as above. Unit-level slugs unverified (see Gaps).
- Free alternative: Khan Academy SAT Math practice (same algebra/geometry skills, timed) [https://www.khanacademy.org/test-prep/v2-sat-math](https://www.khanacademy.org/test-prep/v2-sat-math) (URL reachable; relevance is this researcher's inference).

#### Word Knowledge (WK) - 15 items, 9 min (~36 s per item)
- Official content: "identify the synonym that best matches a given word" / "understand the meaning of words through synonyms." — [officialasvab.com Sample Questions](https://www.officialasvab.com/applicants/sample-questions/); [CEP Counselor Manual p.4](https://prod-media.asvabprogram.com/CEP_PDF_Contents/ASVAB_CEP_Counselor_Manual.pdf)
- Third-party strategy: learn common roots, prefixes, suffixes; break unknown words into parts (example: intro- "within" + spect "to look" = introspect); knowing a root gives a general sense even of never-seen words; write extra words for each part to memorize; read widely; use practice tests to surface unfamiliar words. — [Dummies, Prefixes and suffixes to know](https://www.dummies.com/article/academics-the-arts/study-skills-test-prep/armed-services/prefixes-suffixes-know-asvab-223114) (26 prefixes + 22 suffixes with examples, e.g., a- not, anti- against, bi- two, circum- around, de- away from, hyper- over, re- again, semi- half, tele- far, trans- across, -able capable of, -ful full of, -less without, -ish resembling); [Dummies, Find the meaning of an unknown word](https://www.dummies.com/article/academics-the-arts/study-skills-test-prep/armed-services/how-to-find-the-meaning-of-an-unknown-word-on-the-asvab-150451); [Mometrix](https://www.mometrix.com/academy/asvab-test/)

## Key Question 5: Which subtests matter most for Coast Guard ratings (short note)

### Takeaway
Coast Guard enlistment requires AFQT 32 for high-school graduates (COMDTINST 1100.2I, June 2025, via third-party citation); AFQT itself is AR+MK+WK+PC, so those four dominate. Rating composites seen in official and third-party sources: VE+AR for BM, ME, MK, SK, YN, PA-type ratings; MK+EI+GS (plus a minimum AR) for ET and IT; VE+MK+GS+AR for HS; minimum AR for AMT/EM; minimum MK for MST. All A-school composites were lowered 10 points (except Diver) effective 1 Nov 2021, and current official figures are not published on gocoastguard.com, so a tool should tell students to confirm with a recruiter.

### Cited Findings
- Official Coast Guard (2016 process guide): Storekeeper (SK) A-school requirement "VE+AR = 105 with a minimum VE of 51"; BM example "AR+VE = 100." — [USCG ASVAB Process Guide (PDF, Feb 2016)](https://www.uscg.mil/Portals/3/Documents/VOL_ED/ASVAB_Process_Guide_22FEB2016.pdf) (direct fetch 403; content via search snippets)
- Official Coast Guard (MyCG / PPC, Oct-Nov 2021): "The composite score requirements for all A-school (except diver) have been adjusted by 10 points," effective 01 November 2021; "the specific subtests used to establish a minimum composite score for each rating have not changed"; A-school training and graduation standards unchanged. — [MyCG, ASVAB score requirements changing for A-schools](https://www.mycg.uscg.mil/News/Article/2816942/asvab-score-requirements-changing-for-a-schools/) (403 on direct fetch; via search); [PPC Flag Voice 563](https://www.dcms.uscg.mil/ppc/news/Article/2816424/coast-guard-asvab-policy-changes-flag-voice-563/) (403 on direct fetch; via search)
- Third party (Test-Ninjas, citing TRACEN Petaluma school pages captured 2026 and COMDTINST 1100.2I): Coast Guard AFQT minimum 32 (Tier I HS grad) / 47 (Tier II); ET: MK+EI+GS 172 with AR >= 52, or AFQT 65; IT: MK+EI+GS 172 with AR >= 52 or AFQT 65 (MyCG 2021 commentary gives 162); HS: VE+MK+GS+AR 207 with AR >= 50; ME: VE+AR 100. Notes that school pages may still show pre-2021 (10 points higher) figures. — [Test-Ninjas, Coast Guard jobs by ASVAB score](https://test-ninjas.com/coast-guard-jobs-by-asvab-score)
- Third party (Operation Military Kids, updated Feb 2026): minimums listed as AET 171; AMT 213 with min AR 52; AST 162 with min AFQT 65; BM 101; CS 106; DC 152; DV 104; EM 152 with min AR 52; ET 171 with min AR 52 or AFQT 66; GM 208; HS 154; IT 171 with min AR 52; MK 150 or VE+AR 106; MST 115 with min MK 58; ME 100; OS 106; PA 110 with min VE 60; SK 106 with min VE 52; YN 106 (NO+CS = 101). Composite formulas mostly not named; figures conflict with Test-Ninjas and should be treated as unverified. — [Operation Military Kids, Coast Guard jobs list](https://www.operationmilitarykids.org/coast-guard-jobs-list/)
- Third party (Peterson's): Coast Guard IT requires AR >= 52 and 171 overall; Port Security Specialist 101; Coast Guard AFQT minimum 32; AFQT = WK, PC, AR, MK. — [Peterson's, What jobs do I qualify for](https://www.petersons.com/blog/what-jobs-do-i-qualify-for-with-my-asvab-score-requirements/)
- Third party: gocoastguard.com career pages do not print ASVAB scores; composites are now computed in Direct Access (search summary). — [ASVAB Hero Coast Guard page](https://asvab-hero.pages.dev/coast-guard-asvab-score) (low-reliability, not fetched)

### Inferences
- VE (from WK+PC) and AR appear in nearly every Coast Guard composite cited, so WK, PC and AR are the highest-leverage subtests for a Coast Guard applicant regardless of rating; MK, EI and GS add ET/IT/HS eligibility; AR minimums (50-52) are a gate for AMT, EM, ET, IT, HS. MC and AS did not appear in any Coast Guard composite found here (unlike other services), so they are lower priority unless a rating's school page lists them.

### Gaps
- No current (post-2021) official rating-by-rating composite table was retrievable; mycg.uscg.mil and dcms.uscg.mil returned 403 and gocoastguard.com/careers/enlisted-careers returned 404.

## Key Question 6: Test-day logistics from official sources (ID, calculator, arrival, sleep, PiCAT vs in-person)

### Takeaway
Official: bring valid ID or you will not be admitted; arrive on time or be turned away and rescheduled; no calculator; recruiter cannot enter the test room; CAT session about 2 hours with scores immediately; rest the night before; never share test questions. PiCAT is an unproctored at-home CAT-ASVAB with no subtest time limits, one attempt, first-time testers only, 48 hours to finish, followed by a 25-30 minute proctored verification test within 45 days; in-person at MEPS is timed and proctored but gives official scores same day.

### Cited Findings
- "You'll need to bring valid identification to be admitted into the testing room." "Don't be late - you'll be turned away and required to reschedule if you are." Your recruiter may drive you but "is not permitted in the testing room." "You cannot use a calculator when taking the ASVAB." CAT testing takes about 2 hours; the remaining time and number of questions show in the lower right of the screen; scores are available immediately after the session; a physical exam may be conducted the same day; do not give or receive information about specific test questions (severe penalties). CAT is given at MEPS and most MET sites; paper only at a handful of MET sites (3-4 hours including admin). — [officialasvab.com, What to Expect](https://www.officialasvab.com/applicants/what-to-expect/)
- "Get plenty of rest the night before the test." — [officialasvab.com, Preparing](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1384); [?p=1142](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1142)
- Calculators not allowed because the ASVAB is an employment test measuring hand calculation. — [officialasvab.com FAQs](https://www.officialasvab.com/faqs/); [Myths](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1339)
- PiCAT (official): unproctored CAT-ASVAB taken anywhere with internet; access code from a recruiter (expires 30 days); must be completed within 48 hours of starting; about 2-3 hours; no time limits on individual subtests; one attempt only and only for people who have never taken the ASVAB; scores not shown immediately (recruiter sees them); if scores suggest eligibility, a proctored Verification Test (Vtest) at a MEPS/MET within 45 days, about 25-30 minutes, which gives no score but validates the PiCAT; once verified PiCAT becomes the official score of record; supported browsers Edge/Chrome/Safari/Firefox/Samsung Internet; screen at least 7 inches. — [officialasvab.com, PiCAT](https://www.officialasvab.com/applicants/picat/)
- Retest waits (1 month, 1 month, then 6 months) and 2-year score validity mean the first official attempt counts. — [officialasvab.com Retest Policy](https://www.officialasvab.com/?p=1343); [FAQs](https://www.officialasvab.com/faqs/)
- Third party (Dummies): the day before, pack required items, set an alarm, hydrate, at least eight hours of sleep. — [Dummies study schedule](https://www.dummies.com/article/academics-the-arts/study-skills-test-prep/armed-services/following-an-asvab-study-schedule-164183)

### Inferences
- PiCAT vs MEPS trade-off for a 17-year-old: PiCAT removes subtest time pressure and travel but requires a recruiter's code, a quiet uninterrupted 2-3 hours, a 48-hour window, and a later trip for the Vtest; a large PiCAT-vs-Vtest performance mismatch would invalidate the PiCAT (official text says the Vtest "checks whether performance is consistent"), so the student should take the PiCAT alone and honestly. MEPS in-person gives official scores same day and avoids the second trip.
- Official pages do not state a specific "arrive X minutes early"; a tool should say "arrive on time or early; late arrivals are turned away."

### Gaps
- No official page lists prohibited items beyond calculators (phones, notes, etc. are not addressed on the fetched pages).
- The official site does not state what happens to the PiCAT score if the Vtest is inconsistent beyond "validates" language; details of the retest path after a failed verification were not found.
