## Key Question 1: Where does the table live? Current edition? 2024-2026 revision?

### Takeaway
Through October 2021 the per-rating table was Enclosure (3) of the Performance, Training and Education Manual, COMDTINST M1500.10C (Aug 2017 edition; some official docs mis-cite it as "M1510.10"). The April 2023 total revision, COMDTINST 1500.10D, cancelled M1500.10C and **removed the table from the manual**, pointing instead to an "EPM-2 Class 'A' School Assignments Rating Requirements Listing" posted on the PSC website (CAC-restricted). 1500.10D is still cited as current in Coast Guard messages of July and September 2025; no 1500.10E or 2024-2026 replacement was found.

### Cited Findings
- OFFICIAL [snippet]: COMDTINST 1500.10D (Performance Training and Education Manual (PTEM), April 2023, dated 19 April 2023) states "composite score requirements for all Ratings are contained on the EPM-2 Class 'A' School Assignments Rating Requirements Listing posted to the Coast Guard PSC website" and "The Rating Training Advisory Council (RTAC) for each rating determines the pre-requisite requirements for the A School ... compiled into the EPM-2 A School Assignments Rating Requirements Listing posted to the Coast Guard PSC website." It also states "The Performance, Training and Education Manual, COMDTINST M1500.10C is hereby cancelled" and describes itself as a "total revision to the instruction, reorganizing content, defining Formal Training." — [CI_1500_10D.PDF (blocked; snippet)](https://media.defense.gov/2023/Apr/25/2003207511/-1/-1/0/CI_1500_10D.PDF)

## Key Question 2: The Nov 2021 change (official text)

### Takeaway
ACN 112/21, dated 22 Oct 2021 and effective 1 Nov 2021, lowered every composite in Enclosure (3) by ten points (Diver excepted) while leaving subtest minimums and the AFQT alternative unchanged; the official worked example is IT: MK+EI+GS 172 → 162, AR minimum 52 and AFQT 65 unchanged. Waiver authority rose from 5 to 10 points and now covers subtest minimums (not AFQT).

### Cited Findings
- OFFICIAL: ACN 112/21 text as quoted by PPC: "The composite ASVAB/AFCT score requirement for all ratings listed in Enclosure (3) of REF (A) are adjusted by ten points. All minimums remain unchanged." The notice is dated October 22, 2021, effective November 1, 2021; "'A' school wait lists will not be changed retroactively." — [PPC news article 2819883 (blocked; snippet)](https://www.dcms.uscg.mil/ppc/news/Article/2819883/changes-to-armed-services-vocational-aptitude-battery-asvab-requirements-for-a/)
- OFFICIAL: Flag Voice 563 ("Coast Guard ASVAB Policy Changes"): "All 'A' School ASVAB composite score requirements are adjusted by ten points (except DV)," effective 01 November 2021; "The Coast Guard already grants more than 300 merit-based composite score waivers annually for deserving members to attend 'A' schools." — [PPC news article 2816424 (blocked; snippet)](https://www.dcms.uscg.mil/ppc/news/Article/2816424/coast-guard-asvab-policy-changes-flag-voice-563/)
- OFFICIAL: MyCG commentary "ASVAB score requirements changing for 'A' schools" by Lt. Christie Connell, 22 Oct 2021, labelled ACN 112/21: "The composite score requirements for all A-school (except diver) have been adjusted by 10 points. The specific subtests used to establish a minimum composite score for each rating have not changed." "Composite score (MK+EI+GS) requirement for the information systems technician rating (IT) was 172 and is now 162." "Neither the minimum Arithmetic Reasoning (AR) subtest score of 52, nor the alternate qualification (AFQT) score of 65 have changed." "These are the first major changes to the Coast Guard's ASVAB policy since 2004 and will become effective Nov. 1." "Waiver authority for Coast Guard Recruiting Command Accessions and commanding officers/officers in charge was raised from five to 10 total points and now includes subtest minimums, other than the AFQT." "Today, 12 of 21 A-school waitlists do not have enough qualified applicants to fill even a single convening to capacity, and more than 600 A-school seats have gone empty in fiscal year 2021." Worked example: a member 8 composite points short and 2 AR points short of IT — "The CO/OIC can choose to waive the needed eight points for the composite score and two points for the AR minimum score, so the member qualifies for IT A-school." Also mentions a pilot form to add transparency to the waiver process and that the service will adjust scores up or down based on data; "this policy will not affect those training or graduation requirements." — [MyCG article 2816942 (blocked; snippets)](https://www.mycg.uscg.mil/News/Article/2816942/asvab-score-requirements-changing-for-a-schools/)
- OFFICIAL (research report, DoD-funded) [snippet]: Dynamic Measurement LLC / USCG final report "Improving the Fairness of Coast Guard..." (Dumas, McNeish, Dong, Duellberg; 5 May 2022; DTIC AD1168899) contains tables of per-rating cut-offs and states "Both tables reflect minimum cut-off composites effective November 2021 after the most recent policy change, which cut minimum composite scores by 10 points across rates (USCG, 2021)." It also references a "December 2021 directive that lowered the AFCT requirement from 36 to 32 to open the aperture for 'A' school" [fragmentary snippet]. — [DTIC AD1168899 (blocked)](https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/trecms/pdf/AD1168899.pdf); alt path [AD1168899.pdf](https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/pdfs/AD1168899.pdf)
- OFFICIAL-adjacent (peer-reviewed, USCG data): McNeish, Dumas, Dong & Duellberg, "Promoting Inclusive Recruiting and Selection Into Military Training Schools: Admission Waivers Versus Retesting," Journal of Applied Psychology (2023), DOI 10.1037/apl0001147: the USCG "relies mainly on the ASVAB for assigning active-duty recruits to one of 19 specialized training schools"; data on 16,624 recruits 2013–2021; waiver recipients were less likely to complete A-school on time than those who qualified outright or by retesting. — [ASU Pure record](https://asu.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/promoting-inclusive-recruiting-and-selection-into-military-traini)
- OFFICIAL: ACN 148/20 (Dec 2020, Two-Year Enlistment Program) required "an AFQT score of 36 and ASVAB eligibility for at least one Class A school." — [govdelivery ACN 148/20](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/2b12da7)
- OFFICIAL: gocoastguard.com states "the minimum ASVAB AFQT score for enlisting in the U.S. Coast Guard is 32." — [gocoastguard Get Started](https://www.gocoastguard.com/get-started)
- THIRD-PARTY: prep sites attribute the AFQT floor of 32 (HS diploma) / 47–50 (alternative credential) to COMDTINST 1100.2I §4.B.7 (June 2025) and date the drop from 40 to November 2023; unverified (1100.2I is on media.defense.gov, blocked). — [CI_1100_2I.PDF (blocked)](https://media.defense.gov/2025/Jun/04/2003732605/-1/-1/0/CI_1100_2I.PDF); [asvabhero](https://asvabhero.com/coast-guard-ratings-list)

### Inferences
- The ACN wording "adjusted by ten points" together with the IT example (172→162) and the DTIC phrasing "cut ... by 10 points across rates" establishes that the adjustment was a 10-point reduction for all ratings except DV. There is no indication any rating was raised.
- Since 1500.10D (2023) no longer contains the table and the DTIC tables are inaccessible, the IT row (162 / AR 52 / AFQT 65) is the only post-2021 composite confirmed verbatim from an official source.

### Gaps
- Full ACN 112/21 message text not recovered (govdelivery search did not surface it; dcms/mycg blocked).
- DTIC report tables (the only known official-derived post-2021 full table) could not be opened.

## Key Question 3: Per-rating table — official single-rating statements, pre-2021 table, derived post-2021 values, third-party tables

### Takeaway
Seven ratings have official pre-2021 statements (IT, ET, HS, ME, MST, BM, SK) and they match, row for row, the uncited 19-row "Coast Guard Line Scores" table on Wikipedia's ASVAB article; that table is therefore very probably a transcription of Enclosure (3) of M1500.10C and is the best available proxy for the pre-Nov-2021 official table. Applying the ACN 112/21 ten-point cut yields derived current values (IT's is officially confirmed at 162). Commercial prep-site tables (171/213/152/101/106 family) match neither the official pre-2021 nor the derived post-2021 values and should be treated as unreliable.

### Inferences
- The Wikipedia "Coast Guard Line Scores" table is almost certainly a transcription of Enclosure (3) of M1500.10C: every one of its rows that can be checked against an official statement (BM, ET, HS, IT, ME, MST, SK — seven rows, including subtest minimums and AFQT alternatives) matches exactly, and the "FS" (Food Service Specialist) naming dates it to before the FS→CS rename. It should be presented as "pre-2021 Enclosure (3) values, reproduced via Wikipedia (uncited), corroborated on 7/19 rows."
- The commercial-site family (ET 171/AFQT 66, AMT 213, EM 152, BM 101, YN 106, "NO+CS") is internally inconsistent and is one point off the official pre-2021 values in several rows (171 vs 172, 152 vs 153, 101 vs 100, 106 vs 105), suggesting a corrupted copy of an older table rather than any official edition; it is not the post-2021 table either (which would be 162/143/90/95).
- FORCECOM school pages (ET 172, IT 172, HS 207, ME 100) appear not to have been updated after Nov 2021; a study tool should present the derived post-2021 values with the caveat that the official PSC listing is CAC-only.

### Gaps
- No official source reached gives pre- or post-2021 values for CMS (the coordinator's note of a MyCG CMS article stating AR+VE = 110 could not be located or verified from here), DV, MU, PS, IV, or Reserve-only ratings.

- The DTIC report's post-2021 tables (the only known complete official-derived post-2021 list) were not retrievable.

Six official single-rating statements were recovered (IT post-2021; IT, ET, HS, ME, BM, SK pre-2021), plus MST via an official EPM page cited on Wikipedia; all are listed below with the URL that carries the text.

### Cited Findings
- IT, MK+EI+GS 172→162, AR 52, AFQT 65 — [MyCG 2816942 (Oct 22, 2021)](https://www.mycg.uscg.mil/News/Article/2816942/asvab-score-requirements-changing-for-a-schools/)
- IT, MK+EI+GS 172, AR 52 or AFCT 65, "175/60" guaranteed school — TRACEN Petaluma IT page via [Test Ninjas](https://test-ninjas.com/coast-guard-jobs-by-asvab-score)
- ET, "MK + EI + GS = 172 with AR = 52, or an AFQT = 65"; final SECRET eligibility; normal color perception — [FORCECOM TRACEN Petaluma ET page](https://forcecom.uscg.mil/Our-Organization/FORCECOM-UNITS/TraCen-Petaluma/Training/ET)
- HS, VE+MK+GS+AR 207, AR 50 — TRACEN Petaluma HS page via [Test Ninjas](https://test-ninjas.com/coast-guard-jobs-by-asvab-score)
- ME, "Minimum ASVAB score: VE + AR = 100" — [FORCECOM MLEA ME "A" School page](https://www.forcecom.uscg.mil/Our-Organization/FORCECOM-UNITS/MLEA/ME/); archived 2017 version cited by [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maritime_Law_Enforcement_Specialist)
- BM, "AR+VE = 100", no subtest minimum — [ASVAB Process Guide 22 Feb 2016](https://www.uscg.mil/Portals/3/Documents/VOL_ED/ASVAB_Process_Guide_22FEB2016.pdf)
- SK, "VE+AR = 105 with a minimum VE of 51" — [ASVAB Process Guide 22 Feb 2016](https://www.uscg.mil/Portals/3/Documents/VOL_ED/ASVAB_Process_Guide_22FEB2016.pdf)
- MST, VE+AR 114 & MK 56 — [Wikipedia MST citing uscg.mil/epm/AO/Non-Rate/Rate Requirements.asp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marine_Science_Technician)
- Waiver rules: pre-2021 CO/OIC waiver up to 5 points on the total of two or more subtests, subtest minimums not waivable (2016 Process Guide, URL above); post-2021 waiver authority 10 points including subtest minimums other than AFQT (MyCG, URL above).
- Other official pages checked that state NO ASVAB figures: gocoastguard.com MK and IT job pages; Wikipedia pages for AST, AMT, MK, Rescue swimmer. — [gocoastguard MK](https://www.gocoastguard.com/active-duty-careers/enlisted-opportunities/view-job-descriptions/mk); [gocoastguard IT](https://www.gocoastguard.com/active-duty-careers/enlisted-opportunities/view-job-descriptions/it); [Wikipedia AST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aviation_Survival_Technician); [Wikipedia AMT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aviation_Maintenance_Technician); [Wikipedia MK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machinery_Technician)

### Inferences
- Official single-rating statements exist publicly only for ratings whose FORCECOM school pages list prerequisites (ET, IT, HS, ME); the rest are now only in the CAC-restricted EPM-2 listing.
