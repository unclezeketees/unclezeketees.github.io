How the real test works

  • You cannot change an answer once you move on. Here, Next locks your answer, same as the real thing.
  • If time runs out, the real test scores the questions you did not reach as if you guessed at random, and it applies a heavy penalty for a run of wrong answers at the end. Here they count as wrong. Keep answering real answers to the end.
  • The real test is adaptive, so it gets harder as you do well. This one is not adaptive; it mixes easy, medium and hard.
  • The real test may add 15 unscored tryout questions to 2 to 4 subtests, with extra time. This one does not.

Real score check

Type the standard scores from an official ASVAB or school ASVAB (CEP) score sheet. Each is a number near 20 to 80, average 50. The tool then computes the AFQT exactly the way the Department of Defense does and checks every Coast Guard rating formula.

AS is the combined Auto and Shop score printed on the sheet. VE is optional; if your sheet prints it, enter it, otherwise it is computed from WK and PC.

About the score column. The Coast Guard pulled the per-job score table from its public manual in 2023 and keeps the current one behind a login. Every value below is labeled. "Confirmed" means stated in official text after the November 2021 ten-point cut. "Derived" means the last official or best-attested pre-2021 figure minus 10, as the Coast Guard announced. Third-party websites publish stale copies, so ask a recruiter for the dated current list and compare.
