## Rules (Zeke's)

- PIBS: Plan, Inspect, Build, Ship. Tell him the plan and wait for "go" before anything public.
- No em-dashes anywhere. No emojis in deliverables.
- Never invent URLs, titles or facts. Verify on the live page or leave it out.
- Self-inspect rendered pages at full size before calling anything done.
- Never mention UAW or any official union name. Just "union".
- The public domain imprint is **Owasco Valley Classics** (OVC). "Phoenix Classics" is dead, another KDP publisher has that name. Never show it.
- Psilo Press never appears on Uncle Zeke channels.
- Do not merge to `main` without Zeke's go. `main` is the live site (GitHub Pages, unclezeke.com).
